Company overview

The most intuitive way for AEC teams to work with cost estimates

Archestimator is an AEC cost-estimating and proposal workspace for teams that build itemized commercial offers. It starts with the construction workflows where reusable cost logic, revisions, approvals, and proposal handoff need to stay connected.

Problem

Commercial cost knowledge is still trapped in disposable files.

Teams lose time rebuilding rows, finding supplier notes, checking copied spreadsheets, remembering assumptions, and explaining why the proposal changed. The work is skilled; the workflow is fragmented.

Solution

One connected workspace for quote logic.

Archestimator keeps project scope, estimate sheets, reusable components, supplier context, revisions, shared review, and exports in one browser workspace so each estimate becomes reusable project memory.

Product today

Pre-launch MVP with real workflow depth.

  • Project-based estimate sheets with group and area hierarchy.
  • Reusable component library with variants, brands, suppliers, pricing context, media, and attachments.
  • Inline sheet editing, templates, project charts, shared links, comments, decisions, access controls, and Excel proposal export.
  • AI assistance is deferred to the roadmap and is not a launch claim.

Revision intelligence and deeper proposal comparisons remain roadmap items, not launch claims.

Market

AEC is the market. Proposal-building teams are the entry point.

Initial users are architects, engineers, contractors, estimators, quantity surveyors, fit-out teams, interior designers, and suppliers. Clients, owners, developers, project managers, and procurement are the people who review and approve the proposal side of the workflow. The primary market is the Philippines and Southeast Asia.

Why now

Fast is useful only if the number is still explainable.

Teams already use spreadsheets because they are flexible. Archestimator keeps that working logic visible while adding structure, reuse, review history, and proposal accountability around it.

Business model

B2B SaaS, validated through beta pilots first.

EntrySolo workspace for AEC practitioners producing repeat cost estimates and proposals.
TeamSeats, shared libraries, access control, collaboration, export, and proposal review.
LaterClient-side proposal records, supplier catalogs, quote requests, and portfolio workflows after validation.
