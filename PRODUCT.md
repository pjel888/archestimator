# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Construction professionals — contractors, estimators, and design-build teams — plus adjacent professional services (architects, engineers, consultants) who build itemized commercial quotes, estimates, and proposals from reusable cost libraries. Users spend long, focused work sessions inside the app: building estimate trees, comparing supplier pricing, generating client-ready proposals, and collaborating with team members on shared estimates.

Primary context: full-browser desktop workspace during working hours. Secondary context: project review on laptop; proposal preview and approval on tablet or mobile.

## Product Purpose

Archestimator is a contractor-first workspace for building itemized construction estimates, managing a reusable cost component library, and producing document-ready client proposals. It replaces spreadsheets and fragmented cost databases with a single collaborative surface where teams can estimate faster, source with confidence, and deliver proposals that close.

Success means a contractor opens Archestimator, builds an estimate from reusable components in minutes rather than hours, and sends a proposal that the client understands and trusts.

## Brand Personality

**Precise, architectural, understated.**

The product speaks with quiet confidence. It does not shout, decorate, or pitch. Every interface element earns its place through utility. The visual language borrows from architecture and precision tools — crisp lines, restrained typography, monochrome illustration — without becoming cold or inhuman. The tone is professional and direct: no marketing buzzwords, no empty enthusiasm, no filler copy. When the product delivers a number, that number is trustworthy.

Emotional goal: users should feel competent, focused, and in control. The tool disappears into the work.

## Anti-references

- **No construction clichés**: no blueprint backgrounds, hardhat icons, caution-stripe borders, cartoon workers, or "under construction" metaphors.
- **No enterprise/B2B SaaS tropes**: no oversized hero metrics with gradient accents, no busy data-heavy grids without breathing room, no "streamline/empower/supercharge" copy.
- **No orange brand accent**: orange is reserved for landing-page brand expression only; never in app/workshop UI.
- **No decorative depth**: no heavy drop shadows, glassmorphism, glossy cards, or gradient text. Elevation through thin borders and whitespace.
- **No generic dashboards**: no identical card grids with icon + heading + text repeated endlessly. Each surface serves a specific work purpose.
- **No over-rounding**: app/workshop radius is 3px only. The product should feel precise and architectural, not toy-like.

## Design Principles

1. **Precision over decoration.** Every element earns its place. Remove before adding. If it does not help the user estimate faster, build trust, or close a proposal, it does not belong.

2. **Full-browser workspace.** The app owns the entire viewport. No centered max-width cards, no decorative sidebars. The estimate grid is the primary surface; everything else is contextual support.

3. **Confidence through restraint.** Neutral palette, compact typography (12px max in app), 3px radius everywhere. The tool does not shout. It does not need to — the work speaks for itself.

4. **Efficiency for long sessions.** Controls are compact, keyboard-friendly, and predictable. Spreadsheet-like interactions where they make sense. No animation that slows down repeat actions. Designed for the 8-hour workday, not the 30-second landing-page scroll.

5. **Editorial public, compact private.** Landing pages can breathe with generous whitespace, confident headings, and editorial rhythm. The authenticated app is dense, focused, and efficient. Two visual registers, one product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target: **WCAG 2.1 AA**. Color contrast ≥4.5:1 for body text, ≥3:1 for large text. Keyboard-navigable workspace. Semantic HTML and ARIA labels throughout. Reduced-motion alternatives for all animations. Designed for color-blind users (information never conveyed by color alone). Support for screen readers in critical paths (navigation, estimate editing, proposal review).
