16 Business Model

A B2B SaaS model that expands from solo estimating into team proposal operations.

Who pays

AEC practitioners and teams.

Initial buyers are solo architects, engineers, estimators, fit-out teams, contractors, design-build studios, interior designers, and suppliers that repeatedly produce itemized cost estimates and proposals.

Why they pay

Time saved becomes margin protected.

The value is not another file format. It is faster proposal prep, reusable pricing logic, fewer revision mistakes, clearer client decisions, and less senior time spent rebuilding estimates.

How it expands

From one estimator to the whole proposal workflow.

Start with a workspace subscription, then expand through seats, shared libraries, proposal review, export workflows, and later portfolio workflows for larger AEC accounts.

Entry
Solo workspace

Single practitioner quote builder and reusable component library.

Team
Shared workspace

Seats, presence, version history, shared cost knowledge.

Business
Proposal operations

Client review, approvals, exports, templates, and handoff controls.

Validation
Paid pilots first

No fixed prices claimed until willingness to pay and support burden are proven.
