03 The Problem

Commercial cost knowledge is still trapped in disposable spreadsheets.

  • Quote-heavy teams still build estimates from one-off sheets, copied templates, and scattered supplier files.
  • Every project creates reusable pricing logic, but that knowledge disappears after the proposal is sent.
  • The result is slower offers, inconsistent line items, weak revision traceability, and repeated manual work.

The problem is not only calculation.
It is memory loss.

12 Data Intelligence · Emerging Opportunity

Every estimate can build a living picture of real costs.

  • As teams estimate, Archestimator captures structured, ground-level cost signals: materials, rates, suppliers, and assumptions over time.
  • Aggregated and anonymized, those signals could show how real construction costs move before they appear in formal reports.
  • That creates a long-term path toward benchmarking, research, and broader economic insight.

An emerging opportunity, to be explored responsibly after core product validation. Not a current commercial claim.

14 Business Model

A B2B SaaS model that expands from solo estimating into team proposal operations.

Who pays

Quote-heavy professionals and businesses.

Initial buyers are solo architects, fit-out teams, contractors, design-build studios, suppliers, and small businesses that repeatedly produce itemized commercial offers.

Why they pay

Time saved becomes margin protected.

The value is not another file format. It is faster proposal prep, reusable pricing logic, fewer revision mistakes, clearer client decisions, and less senior time spent rebuilding estimates.

How it expands

From one estimator to the whole proposal workflow.

Start with a workspace subscription, then expand through seats, shared libraries, proposal review, AI drafting, export workflows, and eventually analytics for larger business accounts.

Entry
Solo workspace

Single practitioner quote builder and reusable component library.

Team
Shared workspace

Seats, presence, version history, shared cost knowledge.

Business
Proposal operations

Client review, approvals, exports, templates, and handoff controls.

Validation
Paid pilots first

No fixed prices claimed until willingness to pay and support burden are proven.
