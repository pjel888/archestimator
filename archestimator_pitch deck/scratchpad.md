# Archestimator pitch deck

## Audience / context
General incubator, accelerator, cohort, and early investor reviewers. 4-7 min, 17 slides, HTML source for now.
Tone: premium, founder-led, disciplined, investable, grounded. No hype.
Honesty: pre-launch MVP, bootstrapped, no customers/revenue/funding/partnerships.

## Title style
Eyebrow (mono small-caps "NN / TOPIC") + declarative action headline. Parallel across all slides.

## Title sequence (story told by titles alone)
1. Cover - Archestimator
2. PROBLEM - Commercial cost knowledge is still trapped in disposable spreadsheets.
3. WHY IT MATTERS - Proposal work is where commercial knowledge should compound.
4. THE PRODUCT - A structured workspace for estimates, reusable components, and proposal review.
5. HOW IT WORKS - Connected quote logic, from scope to client decision.
6. COLLABORATION - Teams and clients work from one accountable source of truth.
7. AI ROADMAP - AI is deferred from launch and may return only as a reviewed, human-controlled capability.
8. MARKET - AEC is the market. Construction estimating is the implemented foundation and proposal-building teams are the entry point.
9. ADVANTAGE - A workflow and data model, not a spreadsheet skin.
10. TECHNOLOGY & CONTROL - Human judgment, scoped data, and clear product boundaries.
11. DATA INTELLIGENCE - Every team's estimates build its own reusable cost and supplier context; customer data is not pooled.
12. STATUS - Built enough to validate, early enough to shape with the right cohort.
13. BUSINESS MODEL - A SaaS business, validated through beta pilots.
14. ROADMAP - A six-month validation path to paid pilots.
15. THE ASK - What we need from a serious innovation cohort.
16. FOUNDER FIT - I know the problem because I have lived it.
17. FOUNDER CV - A bridge between architecture, software, design, and business.

Colors: paper #F4EFE8, card #FFF, ink #1B1916, muted #5E574D, line #E4DCD0,
accent #FF7A1A, accent-deep #E95D1A, accent-ink #C24A12.

## Guardrails
No em dashes. No invented metrics/customers/logos. Label planned features (Revision Intelligence,
Proposal Comparisons) as planned. AI is deferred and must not be presented as a current capability. Mock product data = illustrative,
generic items only, never a named client.
