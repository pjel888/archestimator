# Skill: Archestimator Competitive Research

## Use when

- Researching estimating/takeoff/quote/BOQ competitors
- Evaluating npm packages or open-source repos for product inspiration
- Checking market signals from X/Twitter, Reddit, GitHub, npm, or vendor pages

## Required reading

1. `.ai/06_specs/archestimator-product-thesis.md`
2. The FAST PATH in `.ai/10_state/current-state.md`
3. Current source for the product surface being compared

The dated May 2026 competitor, npm, open-source, and social-signal files under
`.ai/13_research/` are historical baselines, not current market evidence. Refresh
pricing, product behavior, package versions, maintenance, and licensing from
primary sources when the task requires current claims.

## Competitor categories

Track competitors by category:

- takeoff + estimating platforms
- contractor estimating + job-management suites
- quote/invoice apps
- cost database / BOQ / QS tools
- residential/remodeler estimating tools
- local/regional Philippine alternatives

## Research rules

- Capture source URL and access date.
- Separate vendor claims from verified product behavior.
- Prefer primary sources and mark inference explicitly.
- Record pricing only when visible from a reliable source.
- For X/Twitter, do not read or expose credential files. Use safe search only.
- For open-source repos, check license before suggesting reuse.
- GPL/AGPL/commercial/unlicensed repos are conceptual inspiration only unless licensing is approved.

## Product lens

Every competitor finding should answer:

1. Does this help contractors estimate faster?
2. Does this solve revisions/design changes?
3. Is it too heavy, too expensive, or too estimator/QS-oriented?
4. What workflow pattern should Archestimator copy conceptually?
5. What gap can Archestimator exploit?
