Top recommendation

Start with candidate 1 — carve the Estimate-tree read module out of the workspace god-file.

It is the single largest file in the repository (4441 lines), the deepening is clean because it is read-side (lower risk than the write paths in candidate 2), and it pays out three ways at once: locality for ~25 callers, a narrow interface that makes the estimate-tree reads testable through it, and two false seams deleted as a bonus (the zero-importer estimateRepository and the no-op clearEstimateRouteContextProcessCache). It also clears the ground for candidate 2 by making the action↔repository layering legible.

If the goal is maximum payoff in the actively-churning area instead, do candidate 3 (the RowMutationCoordinator) next — the estimate-sheet is the hottest belt in the codebase and its row-mutation brain is currently untested because its interface is too wide to exercise.
