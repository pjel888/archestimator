## Guidance (from Doc 53 §2)

- Under NPC Advisory No. 2017-01, PICs and PIPs are encouraged to designate a Data Protection Officer.
- For sole proprietorships or small entities, the owner may serve as the DPO / privacy officer.
- The DPO is responsible for compliance with the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), its IRR, and NPC issuances.
- The DPO should have knowledge of data privacy and information security (or be willing to acquire it).

## Responsibilities

The designated DPO / Privacy Officer is responsible for:

1. **Compliance monitoring** — Ensuring Archestimator's data processing activities comply with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173), its IRR, and NPC issuances.
2. **DSR handling** — Receiving and responding to Data Subject Requests (access, correction, deletion, objection, portability).
3. **Breach response** — Leading breach detection, containment, assessment, and notification per the breach response playbook (doc 54).
4. **Privacy impact assessment** — Conducting or overseeing privacy impact assessments for new data processing activities, including access telemetry.
5. **NPC liaison** — Serving as the point of contact for the National Privacy Commission on all data privacy matters.
6. **Record keeping** — Maintaining records of processing activities, DSR logs, breach logs, and compliance documentation.
7. **Vendor oversight** — Reviewing vendor/processor DPA compliance and data processing agreements.
