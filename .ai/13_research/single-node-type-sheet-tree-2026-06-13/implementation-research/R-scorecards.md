## Judge 1
Verdict: ROUTE 3 wins decisively from the row-loss/rollback lens. The single biggest reason: it is the only route that separates the irreversible forward-only DDL (Phase 5) from the first dangerous mixed-content write (Phase 6) into distinct, independently-revertible releases, while shipping the highest-risk LIVE public share-path firewall (reconstruct-share-tree.ts:297, verified to silently drop rows and understate client-facing totals) FIRST and STANDALONE as a proven prod no-op. At no point can (DB-allows-rows AND a read-gate-still-checks-area) both be true with live writes — the data-loss window simply never opens, and the clean-rollback corridor (Phases 0-5) is the longest of the three. Route 1 is genuinely safe but fuses the migration, writes, templates, and brittle-test rewrites into one unreviewable PR that crosses the no-return line in a single step (deployRisk and reviewability suffer).
