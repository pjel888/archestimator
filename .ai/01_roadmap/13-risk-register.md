# Risk Register

Last updated: 2026-05-30

## Probability & Impact Definitions

| Label | Probability | Impact |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Low | <20% chance in next 6 months | Minor: cosmetic, recoverable, no user harm |
| Medium | 20-60% chance in next 6 months | Moderate: degraded UX, limited scope, fixable within hours |
| High | >60% chance in next 6 months | Significant: blocked workflows, user-visible failure, fixable within days |
| Critical | — | Catastrophic: data loss, legal exposure, security breach, unrecoverable failure |

Severity is the product of probability and impact. "Critical" impact paired with any probability above Low creates a severity of Critical.

### Notes

- **Launch blockers** must be resolved before any public-facing release. These represent regulatory, security, or catastrophic operational gaps that cannot ship.
- **Beta blockers** must be resolved before private beta with real users. Some launch blockers are also beta blockers by definition.
- **Accepted risks** are acknowledged as tolerable for initial launch or beta. They are tracked here for visibility and should be revisited before general availability.
- The most urgent mitigation cluster is R-001 (RLS tests), R-003 (secret scanning), R-006 (legal copy), and R-011 (AI leakage tests) — all are Critical severity and launch-blocking.
- ¹ R-025 is accepted only if owner explicitly defers PDF/immutable snapshot priority.
- ² R-027 is accepted for beta only; becomes a launch blocker for public release.
