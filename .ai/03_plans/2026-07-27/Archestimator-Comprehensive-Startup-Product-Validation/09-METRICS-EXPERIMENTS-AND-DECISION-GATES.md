# Metrics, Experiments, and Decision Gates

**Research status:** `DESK_AUDIT_COMPLETE / MARKET_VALIDATION_PENDING`

## Stage Diagnosis

- `[RATIFIED_CANON | HIGH]` Archestimator is in Problem Proof, despite implementation depth.
- `[CODEBASE_FACT | HIGH]` The product and active Timeline implementation can support learning, but the repository does not contain evidence of paid demand or retained workflows.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` The current bottleneck is qualified market evidence, not feature throughput.

## One Metric That Matters

### Evidence-Backed Paid Repeat Accounts

**Definition:** The count of distinct accounts in one deliberately narrow ICP that, by each account’s precommitted natural-cycle due date:

1. supply a recent, concrete problem incident and a real sanitized artifact;
2. complete the agreed estimating-to-decision workflow on a live or immediately upcoming project;
3. complete a second natural workflow cycle without repeated founder pressure; and
4. pay, or make a dated and attributable purchase commitment at the pre-stated post-pilot price.

**Abbreviation:** `EBPRA`

```text
EBPRA = count(distinct qualified ICP accounts meeting all four conditions)
```

**30–45-day acquisition/economic checkpoint:** From at least three active pilots, at least two accounts must satisfy conditions 1, 2, and 4 above and have a named, dated second natural cycle. This checkpoint is not reported as EBPRA unless the repeat has actually occurred.

**Retention target:** `EBPRA >= 2` from that same cohort by the latest precommitted natural-cycle due date. Record each account’s due date before first-cycle completion from a named project, bid, revision, or scheduled operating event. Recruit the first cohort only when a genuine second cycle is expected within 90 days; otherwise classify the account as a cadence mismatch before enrollment rather than manufacturing a repeat.

- `[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | MEDIUM]` Two accounts are not product-market fit. They are the minimum credible signal that the problem, behavior, repeat value, and budget can coexist in the same narrow segment.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` A count is preferable to an averaged engagement metric at this stage because the sample is intentionally small and the decision is whether to earn the right to expand delivery.
- `[SECONDARY_EVIDENCE | HIGH]` YC, Techstars, SVPG, a16z, and Sequoia all point toward concrete problem behavior, value/business risk, payment, and retention rather than feature completion as decisive early evidence (SRC-F01, SRC-F03, SRC-F05, SRC-F09–SRC-F13).

## Metric Qualification Rules

An account does **not** count if:

- the participant cannot show or reconstruct a recent incident;
- only a founder-created demo dataset is used;
- the workflow succeeds only through unbounded founder administration;
- the recipient never participates when recipient behavior is part of the value promise;
- the second cycle is manufactured solely for the test, lacks a named due date recorded before first-cycle completion, or occurs only after the precommitted due date without a documented external schedule change;
- the commitment is “interested,” a nonbinding compliment, or an undisclosed discount;
- the payer is not a genuine budget holder or approved delegate;
- the price changes opportunistically after every objection; or
- a security, privacy, or record-integrity shortcut is required.

## Countermetrics

| Countermetric | Why it matters | Initial guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Net-new proposer capture time | Timeline can add administration rather than remove it | Median net-new time ≤10 minutes per ordinary change after setup; no session >20 minutes without material offsetting task removal |
| Recipient channel leakage | Portal admiration is irrelevant if decisions return to chat/email | At least 60% of tested decisions/questions occur in the intended workflow during pilots |
| Founder service hours | Free implementation labor can masquerade as product value | ≤3 setup hours/account and ≤30 minutes founder intervention per ordinary second-cycle event |
| Critical comprehension errors | Confusing baseline, proposal, request, or directive creates risk | Zero material state/authority errors in passed sessions |
| Incomplete official records | A living record fails if required moments are skipped | ≥80% of pre-agreed critical fields/events captured without later reconstruction |
| Time to first value | A broad system may delay benefit | First real estimate/proposal workflow completed within one working day after data is available |
| Data/permission incidents | Evidence value cannot justify confidentiality failures | Zero cross-project, cross-portal, or unauthorized artifact exposure |
| External-recipient setup burden | Multi-party products can stall before value | Recipient reaches first review without live founder coaching in ≥80% of trials |
| Estimate-only value | Connected thesis may be false | Track accounts using only estimating versus both stages; do not count estimate-only accounts as connected-workflow proof |
| Repeat-cycle interval | Retention must match workflow cadence | Measure against the account’s natural estimate/revision cadence, not arbitrary DAU/WAU |

The numeric guardrails are precommitted operating thresholds, not validated industry benchmarks. Revise only after a completed research round, with the reason recorded before interpreting the next round.

## Decision Gate Summary

This table is the canonical threshold source for this research cycle. If another artifact abbreviates a gate, this table governs; change it only before a new round begins and record the reason.

| Gate | Minimum evidence | Pass | Fail / inconclusive action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem Proof | 8 qualified interviews in one candidate ICP | ≥5 recent incidents and unprompted active concern; ≥4 artifacts; ≥4 quantified material consequences; ≥4 active workarounds; pattern is substantially the same job | Change/narrow ICP or problem; do not add broad scope |
| Timeline comprehension | 6 uncoached tests: at least 3 proposers and 3 recipients | ≥5 correctly identify the current accepted baseline, pending Issue, what changed, why, supporting evidence, assumptions, exclusions, unresolved consequences, requested decision, and resulting state after acceptance; zero authority errors | Revise vocabulary/model; if repeat failure, keep Timeline supporting-only |
| Timeline capture behavior | 6 realistic capture sessions | ≥5 complete the critical record; median net-new capture time ≤10 minutes; no session >20 minutes without material offsetting task removal; ≥4 replace existing work rather than add duplicate admin | Narrow required fields/events or stop Timeline expansion |
| Recipient behavior | 5 live or high-fidelity sends | ≥4 open uncoached; ≥3 act in workflow; ≤1 comprehension-driven external fallback | Treat portal as owner-side record/export; do not require recipient adoption |
| Assembly language | 8 estimating users across target segment | ≥6 naturally recognize the same distinct reusable concept and correctly predict its behavior; ≥5 select it over Templates or Project Sources for a real task; the task shows a measurable time or accuracy gain | Merge with Templates or Project Sources; do not create first-class Assembly |
| Connected product | 3 active account pilots | ≥2 complete estimate → issue → decision → revision as one natural workflow and return for another cycle | Separate or sequence products; lead with the stage showing value |
| Design partner | 5 qualified asks | ≥3 contribute costly commitment: real project, artifacts, time, introductions, or paid pilot | Revisit urgency/ICP; stop using “design partner” loosely |
| Economic | Identical Offer V1 to 5 comparable budget holders: ₱5,000/30 days, then ₱2,500/month | ≥2 paid or dated purchase commitments to the unchanged package; refunded pilots do not pass | Change value proposition, buyer, package, or stop expansion—not merely lower price |
| Retention | 3 pilots with a named natural second-cycle due date recorded before first-cycle completion | ≥2 repeat by their precommitted due dates, without more than one ordinary reminder and without founder operating the workflow | No product-user fit; diagnose value/capture burden before growth |

## Experiment Backlog

### EXP-01 — Recent Incident Discovery

- **Claim tested:** A narrow segment repeatedly loses time, money, margin, or decision clarity when estimates and official changes fragment.
- **Method:** Eight Mom Test interviews in one segment, each anchored to the last 90 days.
- **Required evidence:** incident timeline, actors, workaround, consequence, buyer, and at least four sanitized artifacts across the set.
- **Pass:** Problem Proof gate above.
- **Disconfirming result:** Incidents are rare, low-consequence, solved adequately, or differ so much that no coherent job exists.
- **Decision:** Choose first ICP and problem, or change them before further product expansion.

### EXP-02 — Alternative and Spend Reconstruction

- **Claim tested:** Customers already “hire” a workaround and spend enough time/money to support a product budget.
- **Method:** For every qualified incident, calculate current software, admin labor, rework, outsourced work, delay, and risk response.
- **Pass:** At least four of eight accounts have an attributable recurring workaround and a plausible budget owner.
- **Disconfirming result:** No active workaround, no owner, or consequences treated as immaterial.
- **Decision:** Reframe economic value or stop the segment bet.

### EXP-03 — Timeline State Comprehension

- **Claim tested:** Users can distinguish current accepted baseline from pending issue and understand the causal record without explanation.
- **Method:** Six uncoached prototype/current-product tasks using realistic content.
- **Pass:** Timeline comprehension gate.
- **Disconfirming result:** Users confuse accepted/proposed/instructed states, read the full chronology to find current truth, or cannot identify requested decision.
- **Decision:** Simplify current-state surface, rename objects, or keep chronology secondary.

### EXP-04 — Capture Burden Shadowing

- **Claim tested:** The official record can be created at acceptable cost because it replaces or consolidates existing work.
- **Method:** Six proposer sessions using real material; compare current process time with Archestimator time and record missing information.
- **Pass:** Timeline capture behavior gate plus no recurring unavailable required field.
- **Disconfirming result:** Net-new admin dominates, critical information is unavailable at publication time, or users defer capture until a dispute.
- **Decision:** Reduce mandatory capture to the smallest truth-preserving package; stop artifact/RBAC/AI expansion not needed for the test.

### EXP-05 — Recipient Channel Test

- **Claim tested:** External reviewers will use a stable portal for at least the decision-critical moment.
- **Method:** Five real/high-fidelity issue sends through normal channels; no live coaching.
- **Pass:** Recipient behavior gate.
- **Disconfirming result:** Recipients demand static PDF/email, respond exclusively in chat, or require the proposer to duplicate everything.
- **Decision:** Make portal optional and owner-side record authoritative; support import/receipt of external decisions rather than forcing behavior.

### EXP-06 — Multi-Artifact Issue Weight Test

- **Claim tested:** Bundling exact estimate, documents, evidence, assumptions, exclusions, and unresolved items increases decision clarity more than it increases publication burden.
- **Method:** Compare a minimal issue and full issue with three proposers and three recipients; measure completion, comprehension, time, and preference based on tasks.
- **Pass:** Full package improves material comprehension for complex cases, while minimal package wins ordinary cases; users can select a clear tier.
- **Disconfirming result:** Full package creates no better decisions or causes abandonment.
- **Decision:** Use progressive disclosure and case-based required fields; do not make every issue a maximal package.

### EXP-07 — Baseline Semantics Test

- **Claim tested:** Separating accepted baseline from pending proposal resolves a real recurring ambiguity.
- **Method:** Incident interviews plus six state tasks.
- **Pass:** At least four problem incidents contain prior/current proposal confusion, and ≥5/6 users correctly use the separated model.
- **Disconfirming result:** Users operate on a different authority model or need contract-specific states not represented.
- **Decision:** Preserve data integrity, but revise product language and domain model before making baseline the lead promise.

### EXP-08 — Assembly Card Sort and Reuse Choice

- **Claim tested:** “Assembly” is a distinct, recognized, valuable estimating object.
- **Method:** Eight natural-language card sorts followed by a realistic estimate-start task.
- **Pass:** Assembly language gate.
- **Disconfirming result:** Participants call it template, build-up, package, system, composite item, or simply copy a prior project, with inconsistent lifecycle expectations.
- **Decision:** `ASSEMBLY_MERGE_WITH_TEMPLATES` unless evidence instead clusters around another explicit model.

### EXP-09 — Price and Packaging Test

- **Claim tested:** A qualified first-ICP buyer will make an economic commitment to the concrete narrow outcome, with free external recipients.
- **Method:** Present the identical Offer V1 from `08-PRIMARY-RESEARCH-KIT.md` to the first five comparable buyers: ₱5,000 for 30 days, one workspace/up to three internal users/unlimited external recipients/one live loop/up to three founder hours, followed by ₱2,500/month. Run any per-project or volume packaging test only in a separately labeled later round.
- **Pass:** At least two paid or dated commitments to the unchanged package, no refund, and delivery/support cost remains plausible.
- **Disconfirming result:** Buyers cannot predict cost, project pricing discourages use, or user pricing blocks collaboration.
- **Decision:** Choose the metric with clearest value/cost alignment; do not infer Philippine WTP from foreign competitor prices.

### EXP-10 — Connected Workflow Pilot

- **Claim tested:** Reusable estimating knowledge and commitment history are one natural product loop.
- **Method:** Three real accounts complete one full loop and a second cycle.
- **Pass:** At least two satisfy the 30–45-day acquisition/economic checkpoint; the connected-product and retention verdict is deferred until those accounts reach their precommitted natural-cycle due dates and at least two satisfy full EBPRA.
- **Disconfirming result:** Different roles, budgets, cadences, or systems own the two stages; one stage is repeatedly skipped.
- **Decision:** Sequence as separate wedges or integrations instead of forcing one product narrative.

## 30-Day Validation Sequence

### Days 1–3: Narrow and Recruit

- Select one candidate ICP: recommended starting hypothesis is **small Philippine contractor or fit-out firm that creates its own estimates and handles client-driven revisions on private projects**.
- Prepare two real incident examples and redacted test fixtures.
- Recruit 30 prospects across founder network, PCAB/industry directories, professional associations, referrals, and direct outreach.
- Do not lead with “living contractual ledger.”

### Days 4–10: Problem and Buyer Proof

- Run eight qualified recent-event interviews in the selected ICP.
- Request artifacts and quantify current workaround/cost.
- Interview at least two economic buyers separately when the user is not the buyer.
- Apply the Problem Proof gate before prototype interpretation.

### Days 8–14: Concept and Language

- Run Timeline comprehension with three proposers and three recipients.
- Run Assembly card sort/reuse choice with eight estimators.
- Revise only terminology and minimum workflow necessary to remove observed comprehension defects.

### Days 12–20: Capture and Recipient Behavior

- Run six capture-burden sessions on realistic material.
- Send five issues through participants’ normal communication path.
- Measure channel leakage and founder intervention.
- Choose minimal versus expanded issue package by observed case complexity.

### Days 18–30: Priced Pilots

- Present the unchanged ₱5,000 Offer V1 to five comparable genuine budget holders.
- Start up to three pilots with real project material and explicit success criteria.
- Capture one complete workflow and schedule the second natural cycle.
- Record pay/decline reasons verbatim.

### Day 30 Decision Review

Do not ask “Did people like it?” Apply the gates:

1. Which problem/ICP passed?
2. Did Timeline comprehension and capture pass independently?
3. Did recipients participate?
4. Did Assembly earn a separate concept?
5. Did two buyers commit at a real price?
6. Does each pilot have a named, dated second natural cycle, and has any account legitimately completed it already?

## Kill, Narrow, and Pivot Triggers

### Remove or Merge Assembly

- Fewer than 6/8 target estimators naturally recognize a distinct concept; or
- fewer than 5/8 choose it over Templates/Project Sources for a real job; or
- lifecycle expectations conflict materially across the first ICP.

### Narrow the Timeline

- Capture gate fails but baseline/proposal comprehension passes; or
- recipients prefer external channels but owners value one preserved record; or
- ordinary changes do not justify multi-artifact packages.

Narrow to: current accepted state, pending proposal, exact issued snapshot, concise change summary, attachments, decision receipt, and external-decision capture.

### Separate Products

- Estimating and commitment stages have different primary users/buyers and fewer than 2/3 pilots complete both naturally; or
- one stage produces value while the other repeatedly delays activation.

### Change ICP or Buyer

- Problem proof clusters in another segment/role;
- user has no budget or switching power;
- formal procurement/security burden exceeds small-account economics; or
- public-infrastructure rules produce a materially different product requirement.

### Pause Broad Delivery

- Problem Proof fails after two materially different qualified segment rounds; or
- no five comparable priced offers can reach genuine buyers; or
- security/legal scope is required before basic value can be tested.

### Pivot or Stop

- Two qualified segment rounds show no frequent material problem with active workarounds;
- paid pilots cannot be obtained after offer and buyer revisions;
- repeat use fails because the workflow is episodic or duplicative; or
- the only valued outcome is already adequately delivered by a dominant alternative at lower switching cost.

## Red Flags and Data Gaps

- **Red flag:** Feature completion can improve technical confidence while leaving EBPRA at zero.
- **Red flag:** A founder-assisted first cycle is not retention.
- **Red flag:** Changing thresholds after weak results would destroy the value of precommitment.
- **Data gap:** The proposed thresholds have not yet been calibrated against actual Archestimator workflows.
- **Data gap:** Natural workflow cadence may exceed 30 days; each pilot therefore needs a named second-cycle due date recorded before first-cycle completion, and the cohort retention verdict occurs only at the latest of those precommitted dates.
