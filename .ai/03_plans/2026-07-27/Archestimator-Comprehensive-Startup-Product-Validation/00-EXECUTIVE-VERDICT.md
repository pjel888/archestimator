## Bottom Line

Archestimator should continue, but not as if the full ratified product thesis has been market-validated.

`[CODEBASE_FACT | HIGH]` The product is unusually substantial for its evidence stage: real estimating, reusable components/templates/project sources, proposal-oriented issue publication, basic client discussion, decision capture, onboarding, and activation instrumentation exist.

`[RESEARCH_GAP | HIGH]` The commercial foundation is still missing: no completed attributable customer interviews, no quantified problem frequency or consequence, no validated ICP or economic buyer, no paid commitment, no non-founder retention cohort, and no observed full connected loop.

`[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` The startup’s binding constraint is no longer the ability to design or build. It is proving that one narrow customer segment repeatedly experiences, adopts, and pays for one connected estimating-to-change outcome.

## The Decision in One Sentence

Enter through a familiar estimating-and-proposal workflow for a tightly screened contractor/fit-out segment; test one real issue → decision → revision loop; keep the ledger as the internal architecture; merge Assembly into Templates unless customer language and behavior prove a separate object; stop broad Timeline expansion until paid repeat behavior passes the gates.

## Why Continue

- `[CODEBASE_FACT | HIGH]` A usable product wedge already exists; this is not a slide-deck startup.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | MEDIUM]` Estimate-native issue history can remove a real handoff between commercial calculation and later change explanation.
- `[EXTERNAL_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE | HIGH]` AEC practice and incumbent products confirm that controlled scope/cost/time change, exact documents, decisions, and history are established needs.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | MEDIUM]` Small-firm workflow focus, deterministic truth, and low recipient friction could form a useful value curve below enterprise suites.
- `[FOUNDER_OBSERVATION | MEDIUM]` The Canon and implementation show sustained domain conviction and ability to execute complex product infrastructure.

## Why Narrow

- `[RESEARCH_GAP | HIGH]` None of the strongest market claims has reached primary customer evidence.
- `[ACTIVE_PLAN | HIGH]` The Timeline pack combines at least a document system, request/change workflow, issue publisher, client portal, decision/baseline model, event ledger, command center, migration program, and AI layer.
- `[CODEBASE_FACT | HIGH]` The broad connected loop still breaks at multi-artifact publication writers, post-scan evidence lifecycle, accepted-baseline creation, event producers/projection, and the human-readable Timeline.
- `[EXTERNAL_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE | HIGH]` Competitors already offer estimating, assemblies, portals, approvals, change orders, and document history; feature breadth is not a wedge.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` Every additional object and role increases encoding and onboarding before the startup knows who buys.

## Final Verdicts

### Overall — `PROCEED_BUT_NARROW`

- **Confidence:** `HIGH`.
- **Decisive evidence:** substantial live wedge; coherent professional problem class; zero direct demand/WTP/retention proof; implementation scope far ahead of customer evidence.
- **Missing evidence:** eight qualifying incident interviews, at least four source-artifact sets, named buyer/budget, recipient behavior, two meaningful paid commitments, and two paid repeat accounts.
- **Immediate action:** use the next 30–45 days for the precommitted Problem Proof, usability, and economic checkpoint; record each pilot’s natural-cycle due date and judge retention only when that date arrives.
- **Stop doing:** broad feature expansion, generalized positioning, category creation, and interpreting local implementation progress as validation.
- **Next gate:** at least two of three qualified paid pilots complete and repeat a real estimating → issue → decision → revision loop.

### Timeline — `TIMELINE_PROCEED_BUT_NARROW`

- **Confidence:** `MEDIUM`.
- **Decisive evidence:** the AEC information model is coherent and partial runtime primitives exist; proactive capture, recipient adoption, baseline value, and repeat use are unproven.
- **Missing evidence:** median capture burden, unaided recipient comprehension, authority interpretation, economic consequence, and second-cycle behavior.
- **Immediate action:** test accepted current state + one pending estimate revision + deterministic delta + selected evidence/assumptions/exclusions/unresolved items + attributable decision.
- **Stop doing:** broad DMS, role taxonomy, command center, exposure management, cutover breadth, and AI before the narrow loop passes.
- **Next gate:** five of six users complete/comprehend the record, three of five recipients act, and two of three paid pilots repeat.

### Assembly — `ASSEMBLY_MERGE_WITH_TEMPLATES`

- **Confidence:** `MEDIUM`.
- **Decisive evidence:** the separate Assembly implementation is already superseded; Templates and Project Sources cover overlapping hierarchy reuse; no customer evidence establishes a distinct ubiquitous object.
- **Missing evidence:** natural terminology by segment, real choice behavior, rate-build-up requirements, propagation expectations, and WTP.
- **Immediate action:** expose small reusable scope packages as a Template subtype while testing examples without labels.
- **Stop doing:** reviving Assembly as a first-class domain model or public differentiator from founder vocabulary alone.
- **Next gate:** at least six of eight target users independently recognize the same distinct reusable concept and correctly predict its behavior; at least five select it over Templates or Project Sources for a real task; the task shows a measurable time or accuracy gain.

## Immediate Resource Allocation

For the next validation cycle:

- **60% primary research and pilot execution:** recruiting, incident reconstruction, artifact collection, usability sessions, priced asks, follow-up.
- **25% minimum product adaptation:** only gaps that block one real estimate → issue → decision → revision loop.
- **10% instrumentation and evidence hygiene:** account-level funnel, repeat cycle, capture time, founder intervention, and paid-commitment register.
- **5% market maintenance:** competitor/source refresh and recruitment-frame maintenance.

Do not allocate a parallel majority to completing the full Timeline plan. That would preserve the current bottleneck.

## Final Desk-Audit Statement

Archestimator has earned the right to run a disciplined market test, not the right to assume the market result. The most favorable honest conclusion is a narrow proceed: preserve the deeper architecture, sell and test a concrete estimating-to-revision outcome, and let paid repeat behavior decide whether the Timeline becomes the central product or supporting infrastructure.
