## Executive Finding

`[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | MEDIUM]` The best first recruitment frame is not “construction.” It is a narrow set of small Philippine contractors and fit-out businesses that repeatedly create client-facing estimates, personally absorb scope ambiguity, and can decide on software without enterprise procurement.

`[RESEARCH_GAP | HIGH]` The repository contains no completed buyer interviews, paid pilots, price acceptance, budget displacement, or segment-level retention. The proposed ICP is a testable starting point, not a market conclusion.

## Proposed First ICP

### Narrow first segment

**Owner-led or estimator-led Philippine general contractors, specialty contractors, and fit-out firms with approximately 3–30 internal staff that:**

- produce at least several client-facing estimates or variation proposals per month;
- rely primarily on spreadsheets plus email, Viber, WhatsApp, Messenger, PDFs, or shared folders;
- have experienced a recent scope/price/version dispute, collection delay, or margin-impacting omission;
- do not have a deeply embedded enterprise construction-management suite;
- have one reachable operator who both feels the pain and can authorize a small monthly or project-based spend;
- can supply a real recent estimate/revision artifact for testing.

This is `[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | MEDIUM]`, chosen because it minimizes buying friction and lets the same person experience estimate creation, issue publication, revision, and commercial consequences.

### Initial subsegments to compare

| Subsegment | Why recruit | Likely pain cadence | Key risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interior fit-out / renovation contractors | Frequent client-driven scope changes; visual and allowance-heavy proposals | Potentially high | Highly bespoke work may reduce reuse value. |
| Specialty trades | Repeated assemblies, rates, and similar proposal structures | Potentially high | They may need takeoff/invoicing more than commitment history. |
| Small general contractors | Own broader estimate-to-change lifecycle | Medium | More stakeholders and project complexity can increase onboarding burden. |
| Independent estimators / QS consultants | Strong estimate and revision expertise; artifact-rich interviews | High estimating cadence | They may not own recipient decisions or collections. |

### Segment decision

- **Recruit first:** owner-led fit-out/interior contractors and adjacent specialty contractors with at least two client-facing estimates/variations monthly and one recent consequential incident.
- **Use as expert/contrast interviews:** QSs, independent estimators, architects, recipients, and economic buyers.
- **Defer as a sales beachhead:** mid-market contractors, developers, PM/CM, and procurement teams until whole-product and authority requirements are evidenced.
- **Keep unresolved:** whether suppliers/fabricators form a better repeated-quotation wedge; test if their revision loop reaches recipient decisions and not only internal quoting.

## Anti-ICP for the First Validation Cycle

- Large contractors already standardized on Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, or a comparable enterprise suite.
- Public agencies or projects requiring formal procurement integration as a prerequisite.
- One-off home renovators and DIY users with no recurring professional workflow.
- Firms whose primary unmet need is BIM takeoff, accounting, payroll, field scheduling, or procurement.
- Prospects unwilling to show a recent artifact or describe a specific incident.
- Friendly respondents who offer general encouragement but have no active project, consequence, or budget.

`[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` Excluding these groups protects the experiment from long sales cycles, false positives, and jobs Archestimator does not currently need to win.

## Trigger Events

Prioritize prospects with a trigger in the last 90 days:

1. A client disputed an extra or claimed it was included.
2. A revised quote used the wrong prior version.
3. Work proceeded before price or scope was clearly approved.
4. An estimator rebuilt scope/rates from a prior workbook.
5. A payment or collection was delayed by missing documentation.
6. A key estimator or project lead handed work to someone else.
7. A growing firm has outgrown one person’s spreadsheet and memory.

`[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | HIGH]` Trigger-based recruiting is more diagnostic than role-based recruiting because it selects for current behavior and consequence.

## Willingness-to-Pay Hypotheses

Test value and packaging before optimizing a public price page.

### Offer A — Precommitted Paid Problem-Proof Pilot V1

- **Customer:** owner-led contractor with a live upcoming estimate and recent revision/approval incident.
- **Price:** `₱5,000` one-time, paid before setup; refund only if a verified Archestimator defect prevents the agreed workflow and no workaround/extension is accepted.
- **Promise:** one company workspace, up to three internal users, unlimited external recipients, one live project, one reusable-source import, and one estimate → issue → decision → revision cycle.
- **Service boundary:** up to three founder hours for setup/training/ordinary support; no custom integration, bespoke feature, broad data cleanup, legal advice, or founder operation of the customer workflow.
- **Term:** 30 days.
- **Commitment test:** payment or a signed/datable commitment to the identical amount/package with named approver and start date. Refunded pilots do not pass.
- **What it measures:** urgency, services tolerance, onboarding cost, repeat behavior, and economic-buyer identity.
- **Evidence status:** `[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | LOW]`; this is an experiment, not validated or public pricing.

### Offer B — Core estimating/proposal subscription

- **Precommitted post-pilot offer:** `₱2,500/month` per company workspace, up to three internal users, unlimited external recipients, monthly cancellation, standard product support.
- **Pricing metric hypothesis:** company workspace, not external recipient seats.
- **Rationale:** external-seat charges would directly tax the behavior the product needs to create.
- **Evidence status:** `WORKING_HYPOTHESIS`.

### Offer C — Commitment-control add-on or later tier

- **Packaging hypothesis:** accepted baseline, revision delta, evidence packet, and decision history become a higher-value tier only after repeated use proves they are a separate paid outcome.
- **Risk:** packaging the ledger separately too early may hide the connected advantage or create a second-product burden.
- **Evidence status:** `OPEN_DECISION`.

## WTP Interview Rules

Do not ask “Would you pay?” Use behavioral tests:

- What do you pay today for estimating, project management, spreadsheets, storage, or admin labor?
- Who approved that spend and what budget did it come from?
- What did the last scope/version incident cost?
- Before revealing Offer V1: what monthly amount would feel acceptable/good value, expensive but still worth considering, prohibitive, and too cheap to trust? Why?
- After revealing Offer V1: if this pilot starts next week, can you approve `₱5,000` now?
- If not, what exact procurement, proof, or product condition blocks it?
- What would you stop paying for or stop doing if this worked?

Compliments, survey intent, waitlist entries, and hypothetical price ranges are not positive WTP evidence.
