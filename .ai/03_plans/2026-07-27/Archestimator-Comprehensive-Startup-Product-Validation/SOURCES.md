## Source Limitations

- `[RESEARCH_GAP | HIGH]` No supplied source contains completed, attributable customer interviews, support-ticket evidence, pilot observations, usage cohorts, retention, churn, win/loss data, or paid commitments for Archestimator.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` Vendor capability pages show that estimating, client portals, change orders, document history, approvals, and assemblies are already established category features. They do not prove that Archestimator cannot win a narrower segment.
- `[RESEARCH_GAP | HIGH]` Public prices are reference anchors in other markets and currencies. They are not Philippine willingness-to-pay evidence.
- `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` AEC professional sources justify rigorous scope/cost/time/document capture, but they do not certify that Archestimator records are legally binding, contract-compliant, or admissible.

## Red Flags and Data Gaps

- **Red flag:** Internal architecture and framework guidance substantially exceed customer-derived evidence.
- **Data gap:** No first-ICP demand dataset, buyer map, or paid pilot evidence exists.
- **Data gap:** No direct evidence establishes that recipients will leave email/Viber/WhatsApp to use a portal.
- **Data gap:** No natural-language evidence establishes “Assembly” as a distinct user concept.
