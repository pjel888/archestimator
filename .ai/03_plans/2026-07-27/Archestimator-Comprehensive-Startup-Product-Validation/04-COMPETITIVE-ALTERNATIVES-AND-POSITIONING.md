## Executive Finding

`[EXTERNAL_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE | HIGH]` Archestimator is not entering an empty market. Estimating, assemblies, proposals, change orders, portals, document history, approvals, and revision tracking are established capabilities across specialist and integrated construction products.

`[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | MEDIUM]` The opportunity is not to claim that no one manages construction changes. It is to make one narrow workflow materially easier for smaller firms: create a credible estimate, issue the exact commercial position, preserve the accepted state, and explain the next change without enterprise-suite overhead.

`[OPEN_DECISION | HIGH]` “Living contractual ledger” is presently stronger as an internal design principle than as public category language. Customers may recognize “know exactly what changed and what was approved” while rejecting or misunderstanding “ledger,” “contractual,” or “commitment record.”

## The Real Competitive Set

The primary competitor is the customer’s current system, not only named software.

| Alternative | Why customers use it | Strength | Opening for Archestimator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spreadsheet + PDF + chat/email | Familiar, flexible, already paid for, recipient needs no account | Near-zero switching cost and unlimited exceptions | Preserve flexibility while making issue, baseline, revision, and decision explicit. |
| Shared folders + filename/version discipline | Easy document distribution and archive | Universally understood | Add deterministic relationships and current-state explanation. |
| Accounting/invoicing software | Tied to money collection and existing finance habit | System of financial record after approval | Own the commercial truth before it becomes an invoice. |
| Generic document/e-sign tools | Reliable sending and signatures | Low recipient friction | Bind the decision to estimate structure and later delta. |
| Specialist estimating/takeoff tools | Deep quantity, takeoff, rates, assemblies, export | Strong daily estimator value | Compete only where workflow and reusable knowledge are sufficient; avoid pretending to match deep takeoff. |
| SMB construction suites | Estimates, change orders, portals, schedules, financials | Broad integrated value | Offer a faster, more focused estimating-to-decision workflow with lower setup burden. |
| Enterprise construction platforms | Formal cost/change/document controls and integrations | Governance, breadth, ecosystem | Serve firms below enterprise complexity or teams needing a lightweight upstream wedge. |

## Competitor Landscape by Customer Job

### Specialist estimate construction

- **STACK** and **PlanSwift** emphasize takeoff and estimating; public prices show specialist value can support meaningful spend (`SRC-C10`–`SRC-C12`).
- **RIB CostX** offers QS-oriented estimating, rate linkages, and revision capability (`SRC-C13`).
- **Buildxact** and **Houzz Pro** combine estimating/assemblies with broader SMB workflows (`SRC-C08`, `SRC-C09`).

**Implication:** `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` Archestimator should not position on “digital estimating” alone. It must prove either a uniquely low-friction local workflow, superior reuse/traceability, or a tighter estimate-to-decision loop.

### Client review, approvals, and change orders

- **Buildertrend** supports change-order preview and client approval (`SRC-C04`).
- **JobTread** offers client portals, document history, budget-derived change orders, attachments, and digital decisions (`SRC-C05`–`SRC-C07`).
- **Knowify** provides a client portal for trade contractors including change orders and invoices (`SRC-C14`).

**Implication:** `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` A portal and digital approval are category expectations, not defensible differentiation by themselves.

### Formal cost and change control

- **Procore** connects change events/orders to financial management, approvals, budgets, and field capture (`SRC-C01`).
- **Autodesk Construction Cloud** distinguishes potential change orders, cost items, requests, owner/supplier change orders, and related issues/RFIs (`SRC-C02`, `SRC-C03`).
- **Bluebeam** participates through document/revision/RFI/change-order workflows (`SRC-C15`).

**Implication:** `[EVIDENCE_BACKED_INFERENCE | HIGH]` The active Timeline plan’s distinct object types are directionally aligned with professional products, but implementing an enterprise object model does not create an SMB adoption advantage.

## Where Archestimator Can Be Different

### Plausible differentiators to test

1. **The estimate is the origin of the record.** The accepted commercial baseline and future delta derive from the same structured estimate rather than a detached document workflow.
2. **Deterministic explanation, not AI interpretation.** Users can see exact changed scope, quantities, rates, assumptions, exclusions, and decisions before any optional AI summary.
3. **Reusable knowledge remains inspectable.** Templates/components/project sources accelerate work while retaining provenance and local control.
4. **Recipient friction stays low.** One exact issue can be reviewed without buying external seats; notifications can coexist with email/chat habits.
5. **SMB-sized setup.** The product wins only if a real project reaches first value in minutes or hours, not a multi-month implementation.

All five are `[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | MEDIUM]` as differentiators. They require comparative user evidence.

### Non-differentiators

- “All-in-one construction management.”
- A generic customer portal.
- Digital estimates or PDFs.
- Change orders and approvals as feature names.
- Activity feeds, comments, file attachments, or audit logs.
- AI summaries without trustworthy underlying records.

These are either incumbent strengths or expected table stakes.

### Best current entry category

**Construction estimating and proposal workflow for small contractors.**

This is recognizable, matches live product behavior, and provides a credible path to the deeper product. It is not the final strategic ceiling.

### Recommended provisional positioning statement

> For small contractors and fit-out teams who revise client scope and pricing across spreadsheets, PDFs, and messages, Archestimator is an estimating-to-approval workspace that keeps the exact issued number, accepted baseline, and next change connected—so the team can move faster and show what changed without reconstructing the story.

**Status:** `[WORKING_HYPOTHESIS | MEDIUM]`. Test the words “accepted,” “approval,” “connected,” and “show what changed” in interviews. Do not assume “contractual ledger” is customer language.

## Crossing-the-Chasm Beachhead Test

A beachhead is credible only if the first segment shares:

- the same high-consequence use case;
- reachable peer references;
- similar artifacts and workflow;
- one complete product requirement;
- a buyer with comparable budget and authority.

The proposed small-contractor/fit-out frame is still too broad until interviews identify a tighter cluster. A possible result is “owner-led interior fit-out contractors issuing multiple client variations per month,” but this must emerge from incident evidence rather than be declared.

## Claims to Avoid Until Proven

- “The source of truth for construction commitments.”
- “Legally binding” or “contract compliant.”
- “Prevents disputes” or “eliminates margin leakage.”
- “Built for all contractors, estimators, QSs, architects, owners, and DIY users.”
- “Replaces Procore/Autodesk/Buildertrend.”
- “AI that knows your business.”
- “Philippines’ leading” or any market-leadership language.

These claims exceed current evidence or create legal/commercial implications the product has not established.

## Positioning Decision

- **Category:** enter through recognizable estimating/proposal workflow language; keep “living contractual ledger” internal.
- **Competitive focus:** beat spreadsheet/PDF/chat reconstruction on one real revision loop, not every construction platform.
- **Confidence:** `MEDIUM`.
- **Decisive evidence:** live estimate/reuse surfaces; professional need for controlled change; crowded incumbent feature set; absence of customer-language proof.
- **Missing evidence:** comparative switching interviews, lost-deal reasons, chosen alternatives, recipient behavior, and paid conversion.
- **Immediate action:** test the promise hierarchy and a real estimate-to-revision scenario with one tight segment.
- **Stop doing:** broad “cost intelligence” or “all construction roles” positioning until one beachhead repeatedly buys.
- **Next gate:** five of eight interviews must produce recent qualifying incidents, then two of five comparable buyers must accept the unchanged ₱5,000 Offer V1.
