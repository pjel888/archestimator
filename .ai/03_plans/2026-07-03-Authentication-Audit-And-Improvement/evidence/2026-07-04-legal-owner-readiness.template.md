# Legal Owner Readiness Input Template

**Source artifact:** `2026-07-04-legal-owner-readiness.template.json`

This is a sanitized operator handoff template for the legal/public-launch
readiness gate. It lists the proof flags and labels required by
`scripts/check-legal-owner-readiness.ts`.

reports `legalOwnerReady=false` until real operator/legal inputs are supplied
and the checker passes with all required flags and labels.
