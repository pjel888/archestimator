# Compact Page Spine

## Overall rhythm

The landing page is one connected experience, not a sequence of independent marketing bands.

### 1. Hero + Living Estimate — 100svh

Purpose: category clarity, emotional hook, CTA, immediate visual intrigue.

Content:

- Small category line.
- One short H1.
- One two-line supporting sentence.
- Primary CTA.
- Living Estimate canvas occupying the right or lower visual field.
- Tiny four-state index: `Estimate · Proposal · Review · Revision`.

Behavior:

- Scene assembles once on entry.
- The CTA remains visible without scrolling.
- No secondary CTA.
- No feature list.

### 2. Scroll-controlled product story — 170–210svh

Purpose: show the full product idea with one persistent scene.

The canvas pins while four short text beats crossfade:

1. **Build the number.**
2. **Carry it into the proposal.**
3. **Let feedback land on the cost.**
4. **Revise without losing the reason.**

Each beat is one heading plus at most one sentence. The scene changes continuously; do not create four separate cards or screenshots.

### 3. Why it matters — 65–80svh

Purpose: convert the visual story into three plain benefits.

Use one horizontal typographic composition, not cards:

- Less rework.
- Clearer proposals.
- Better cost decisions.

Below it, one concise line explains that Excel can remain in the workflow and that Archestimator connects the work around it.

Optional: one real founder sentence or product-status line. Do not create a separate founder, trust, status, and FAQ section.

### 4. Early access close — 65–80svh

Purpose: make the next action obvious.

Content:

- Short closing headline.
- One honest stage sentence.
- Primary CTA.
- Compact footer navigation.

No FAQ accordion on this page. Link to a separate FAQ or contact route if needed.

## Header

- Logo.
- `Sign in` text link.
- Primary CTA.
- No announcement bar unless there is a genuinely time-sensitive announcement.
- No anchor navigation is required for a four-section page.

## Height budget at 1440 × 900

- Header and hero: 900–1,000px.
- Scroll story: 1,600–1,900px.
- Benefits/trust: 600–700px.
- Close/footer: 650–750px.
- Total target: 3,750–4,100px.

Anything materially above 4,200px fails the brief.
