### Revise without losing the reason.
Use the preserved trail to guide the next edit; Archestimator never changes the estimate for you.

## Copy rules

- No sentence over 24 words unless clarity requires it.
- No paragraph over two lines at desktop body width.
- Do not repeat the full workflow more than twice.
- Do not list audiences in the hero.
- Do not list more than three benefits.
- Do not explain claim boundaries in marketing prose; enforce them in the implementation and supporting pages.
- Do not use `reusable cost intelligence` more than once on the page.
