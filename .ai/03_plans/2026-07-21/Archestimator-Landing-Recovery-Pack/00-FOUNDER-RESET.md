# Founder Reset — `/new`

## Controlling outcome

Make `/new` immediately understandable, visually memorable, and compact enough that a qualified AEC visitor reaches the final CTA without feeling they have read a product manual.

The page should communicate one idea:

> The estimate should remain connected as it becomes a proposal, a client conversation, and a revision.

## Hard constraints

1. Maximum **four main narrative sections plus footer**.
2. Maximum **260 visible words before the footer**, excluding legal navigation.
3. Maximum desktop document height at 1440 × 900: **4,200px**. Target: **3,400–3,900px**.
4. One primary CTA: **Request early access**.
5. No fake application screenshots, fake browser windows, faux dashboards, capability grids, bento cards, customer logos, testimonials, metrics, or traction claims.
6. One purposeful Three.js scene may carry the hero and scroll narrative.
7. Motion must explain the estimate-to-proposal-to-review-to-revision workflow. Decorative movement is removed.
8. Product copy remains honest about the pre-release product and user-controlled workflow.
9. Mobile gets a designed static/SVG or lightweight CSS fallback; it must not be a broken desktop canvas shrunk to fit.
10. The copy and CTA must render before the scene bundle loads.

## Explicitly superseded

The following constraints from the current plan are revoked:

- Attio one-for-one band parity.
- The 18 visible-band contract.
- The 15-main-block invariant.
- The ban on Three.js, WebGL, and GSAP for `/new`.
- The requirement to explain every current capability and proof boundary on the landing page.
- The requirement to create multiple pseudo-product scenes from illustrative fixtures.
- The idea that broad AEC relevance requires broad page coverage.

## Preserve

- Canonical Archestimator logo/wordmark.
- Signal orange `#F24405` and accessible dark orange `#C63804`.
- Construction-estimating category clarity.
- The connected estimate → proposal → review → revision → decision story.
- One primary conversion goal.
- Product truth, accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, and static-first rendering.
- Honest early-access language.

## Design principle

**Broad market, narrow page.**

The homepage does not need to enumerate everyone in the AEC cost-decision network. It needs to make one shared pain and one shared transformation unforgettable.
