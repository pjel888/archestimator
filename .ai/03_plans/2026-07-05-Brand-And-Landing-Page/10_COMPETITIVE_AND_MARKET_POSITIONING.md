## 1. The competitive landscape

Per `MASTER §2` (researched and ratified in v3.0):

### Enterprise estimating (US-centric, enterprise-priced)
- **Procore** — full construction management suite, includes estimating. US enterprise. Stop at estimating/bidding.
- **DESTINI Estimator (Beck Technology)** — centralized historical cost database; "tell a story about your estimate". US enterprise.
- **Ediphi** — saves completed projects into searchable cost history. Cost-prohibitive for solo estimators.
- **InEight Estimate** — benchmarks new estimates against historical/as-built data. Enterprise.
- **Cubicost / CostX (RIB)** — QS-grade tools. Enterprise. Complex setup.
- **RSMeans Data Online** — historical + predictive cost data + Flash AI estimating. US reference data.
- **Zebel** — blends anonymized cross-customer market data with your project history. (Opposite of Archestimator's R10 privacy stance.)
- **ConWize** — price benchmarks from previous-project data.

### Takeoff / measurement
- **Bluebeam Revu** — PDF markup + takeoff. Not a cost library.
- **PlanSwift** — takeoff + estimating. Mainly trade-specific.
- **Stack Construction Technologies** — cloud-based takeoff + estimating.

### SMB / spreadsheet alternatives (direct or adjacent)
- **Clear Estimates** — remodeling-focused. US SMB.
- **Kreo** — UK-based takeoff + estimating. SMB.
- **Countfire** — electrical estimating. Niche.

### Adjacent (not direct competitors but comparison points)
- **Excel / Google Sheets** — the actual status quo for SEA SMB.
- **Notion / Airtable** — generic workspaces some teams press into estimating service.

## 2. The four defensible wedges (from `MASTER §2`, summarized in `08` §6)

| Wedge | Why defensible |
|---|---|
| W1: Fit, reach, price | Built for spreadsheet-bound SEA SMB. None of the enterprise tools serve this segment. |
| W2: Proposal-to-approval + 2-sided client record | Most competitors stop at estimating/bidding. Less commoditized. |
| W3: Supplier-linked bridge | Estimate → supplier catalog → quote request → procurement. Nobody has built this. |
| W4: Private by design | Deliberate opposite of Zebel's anonymized benchmark. Clean privacy story. |

## 4. Per-segment directed messaging (`MASTER §12.10`)

Each AEC persona gets a directed message in the rebuilt Specialties section (`06` §4.8, `08` §7). The messages are calibrated against what enterprise competitors say vs. what Archestimator actually delivers:

| Persona | Enterprise competitor message | Archestimator directed message |
|---|---|---|
| Architect | (rarely served directly by enterprise) | "Your design intent, fully costed. From first concept to client sign-off, every finish option and every exclusion stays attached." |
| Engineer | InEight / DESTINI: "Discipline-specific assemblies, historical benchmarks." | "Build reusable assemblies per discipline. Variant costing, supplier context, and labor logic move with the assembly." |
| GC | Procore: "Full construction management suite." | "Organize costs across phases, locations, trades, bid packages, and allowances. Preserve the assumptions behind the total." |
| Specialty/fit-out | (underserved) | "Build reusable assemblies, variants, supplier references, labor logic, and scope packages that move into future estimates." |
| QS/cost estimator | CostX / Cubicost / RSMeans: "Reference cost data + QS discipline." | "Structure BOQ-grade estimates with unit-rate discipline. Every rate keeps its source note, every quantity keeps its assumption." |
| Interior designer | (underserved) | "Keep rooms, finishes, options, exclusions, and client decisions connected from the first estimate through final approval." |
| Project manager | Procore / PlanGrid: "Project management + drawing markup." | "Walk into every pricing conversation with the full record: what was proposed, what changed, what was approved, when, and by whom." |
