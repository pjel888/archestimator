# Lawful Basis and Consent Matrix

## Principle

Consent must not be used where processing is genuinely necessary to provide a requested service. Consent must also not be bundled with unrelated processing.

## Initial Matrix

| Processing | Primary basis | Additional requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Account creation | Contract | Account privacy notice |
| Authentication | Contract and legitimate interest | Security notice |
| Fraud and abuse prevention | Legitimate interest | Balancing assessment |
| Required transactional email | Contract | No marketing content |
| Collaboration invitation | Contract or legitimate interest | Invitee notice and customer warranty |
| Project and estimate processing | Customer instruction / contract | Processor terms |
| Public share link | Customer instruction | Share-page notice |
| Client comments and approvals | Contract or consent depending relationship | Explicit notice |
| Support | Contract and legitimate interest | Support notice |
| Product analytics | Legitimate interest or consent depending technology and intrusiveness | Balancing assessment and cookie controls |
| Marketing email | Consent or approved lawful basis | Evidence and unsubscribe |
| Legal acceptance records | Contract and legal interest | Immutable version record |
| Security logs | Legitimate interest | Retention and access restrictions |
| AI assistance requested by user | Contract | Provider disclosure and data-use controls |
| AI model training on customer data | Separate specific consent or separate agreement | Default off |
| Payment and tax records | Contract and legal obligation | Retention schedule |
| Privacy request handling | Legal obligation | Identity verification |

## Consent Requirements

Where consent is used, record:

- data subject;
- purpose;
- data categories;
- notice version;
- affirmative action;
- timestamp;
- collection interface;
- withdrawal method;
- withdrawal timestamp;
- effect of withdrawal.

## Legitimate Interest Assessment

Each legitimate-interest activity must document:

1. purpose test;
2. necessity test;
3. balancing test;
4. safeguards;
5. opt-out or objection handling;
6. approval and review date.

## Prohibited Patterns

- pre-checked consent;
- forced marketing consent;
- vague “improve our services” authorization;
- combining sensitive-data consent with general terms;
- treating silence or continued browsing as consent;
- using customer data for model training by default.
