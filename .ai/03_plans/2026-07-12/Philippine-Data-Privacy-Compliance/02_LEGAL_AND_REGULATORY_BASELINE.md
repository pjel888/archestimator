# Legal and Regulatory Baseline

## Applicable Framework

The primary Philippine framework includes:

- Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012;
- the Implementing Rules and Regulations of RA 10173;
- applicable National Privacy Commission circulars, advisories, guidelines, decisions, and registration requirements;
- contractual and sector-specific obligations that apply to Archestimator customers;
- foreign privacy laws where Archestimator intentionally serves residents of other jurisdictions.

## Core Data Privacy Principles

Every processing activity must satisfy:

1. **Transparency**  
   The data subject must understand what is processed, why, how, by whom, for how long, and with whom it is shared.

2. **Legitimate purpose**  
   Processing purposes must be specified, lawful, declared, and compatible with later use.

3. **Proportionality**  
   Collection, access, disclosure, and retention must be adequate, relevant, and not excessive.

## Lawful Processing

Each processing activity must have a documented lawful condition. Typical conditions for Archestimator include:

- consent;
- necessity for contract or pre-contractual steps;
- legal obligation;
- vital interests;
- public authority or emergency, where applicable;
- legitimate interest, subject to a balancing assessment.

Sensitive personal information and privileged information require a stricter basis and safeguards.

## Data Subject Rights

The operational program must support:

- right to be informed;
- right of access;
- right to object;
- right to rectification;
- right to erasure or blocking, where applicable;
- right to damages;
- right to data portability;
- right to lodge a complaint with the NPC.

## Security Obligations

Archestimator must implement reasonable and appropriate:

- organizational measures;
- physical measures;
- technical measures.

Security must account for the nature of the information, risks, organization size, processing complexity, current security practices, and cost of implementation.

## Accountability for Processors and Transfers

The controller remains accountable when processing is delegated. Processor and transfer arrangements must provide sufficient guarantees regarding confidentiality, authorized use, security, deletion, assistance, breach reporting, and data-subject rights.

## Breach Notification

The breach process must determine whether an incident is notifiable and support notification within the legally required period when notification criteria are met. The operational target must be immediate escalation and a 72-hour decision and notification workflow for notifiable breaches.

## Registration and DPO

Archestimator must:

- appoint a DPO or designated privacy officer with documented authority;
- assess whether the organization and its data processing systems must be registered with the NPC;
- maintain the assessment even when registration is determined not to be mandatory;
- update registration when systems or processing materially change.

## Compliance Rule

No product decision may rely only on “industry standard” or GDPR terminology. The actual requirement must be mapped to Philippine law and applicable NPC guidance.
