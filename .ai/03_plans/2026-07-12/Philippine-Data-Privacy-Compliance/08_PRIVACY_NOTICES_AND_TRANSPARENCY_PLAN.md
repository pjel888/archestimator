# Privacy Notices and Transparency Plan

## Required Notice Set

1. Platform account privacy notice
2. Collaboration invitation privacy notice
3. Public share-page privacy notice
4. Password-protected share privacy notice
5. Client comment, approval, and decline notice
6. Upload privacy notice
7. Support privacy notice
8. Cookie and analytics notice
9. AI assistance notice
10. Billing and payment notice when enabled
11. Recruitment and contractor notice when applicable

## Mandatory Content

Each notice must clearly state:

- identity and contact details of the PIC;
- DPO or privacy contact;
- data categories;
- source;
- purpose;
- lawful basis;
- processing methods;
- recipients or recipient classes;
- processors and international processing;
- retention period or criteria;
- rights and request method;
- complaint right;
- automated processing where applicable;
- consequences of not providing required data;
- effective date and version.

## Product Placement

Notices must appear at or before collection:

- registration form;
- OAuth and magic-link entry;
- invitation acceptance;
- public share access where telemetry is collected;
- comment and approval submission;
- upload;
- support form;
- analytics preference center;
- AI feature activation.

## Versioning

Implement:

- immutable notice versions;
- effective dates;
- material-change classification;
- notice acceptance records only where acceptance is legally required;
- prior-notice archive;
- user notification workflow for material changes;
- mapping from processing activities to notice versions.

## Content Accuracy Gate

Legal text must match actual technical behavior, including:

- hashed IP and user-agent values;
- Cloudflare processing;
- email providers;
- analytics and error monitoring;
- AI providers;
- logs and retention;
- backup limitations;
- public-link telemetry;
- customer-controlled content.
