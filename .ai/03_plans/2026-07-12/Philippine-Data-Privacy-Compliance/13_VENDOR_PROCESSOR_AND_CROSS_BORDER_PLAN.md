# Vendor, Processor, and Cross-Border Plan

## Vendor Inventory

Inventory all providers, including:

- Cloudflare;
- email provider;
- Google OAuth;
- AI providers;
- analytics;
- error monitoring;
- support tools;
- payment provider when enabled;
- GitHub;
- domain and DNS providers;
- remaining Supabase or Vercel resources;
- contractors with production or customer-data access.

## Due Diligence

For each vendor, collect:

- service and processing purpose;
- role;
- data categories;
- regions;
- subprocessors;
- security certifications;
- encryption;
- access controls;
- incident history;
- breach notification terms;
- retention and deletion;
- government-access policy;
- audit reports;
- business continuity;
- termination and export capability.

## Required DPA Terms

- subject matter and duration;
- nature and purpose;
- data categories and subjects;
- documented instructions;
- confidentiality;
- security;
- subprocessor controls;
- data-subject assistance;
- breach reporting;
- PIA and regulator assistance;
- deletion or return;
- audit evidence;
- cross-border processing;
- termination assistance;
- liability allocation.

## Subprocessor Register

Publish or provide:

- provider;
- purpose;
- processing location;
- effective date;
- relevant service;
- change-notification process;
- customer objection process where contractually offered.

## Cross-Border Transfer Record

For every overseas processing flow, document:

- exporting entity;
- importing entity;
- countries;
- purpose;
- data;
- legal and contractual safeguard;
- security controls;
- onward transfer;
- retention;
- government-access risk;
- data-subject notice.

## Provider Exit

Maintain an exit plan covering:

- data export;
- migration;
- credential rotation;
- deletion confirmation;
- DNS and routing;
- residual logs;
- backups;
- subprocessor termination;
- evidence preservation.
