# Privacy Impact Assessment Plan

## PIA Governance

A PIA is mandatory for the platform baseline and must be screened for every material feature, provider, data-use change, or access expansion.

## Platform PIA Scope

Include:

- accounts and authentication;
- workspace and corporate accounts;
- collaboration;
- project and estimate data;
- public sharing;
- client review and approvals;
- activity history;
- uploads;
- exports;
- support;
- analytics;
- AI;
- admin;
- retention;
- processors;
- cross-border storage;
- incident response;
- deletion and backups.

## PIA Method

1. Define project and owners.
2. Identify processing purpose and role.
3. Map data flows.
4. Identify data subjects and categories.
5. Validate lawful basis.
6. Test necessity and proportionality.
7. Identify threats and harms.
8. evaluate likelihood and impact.
9. identify existing controls.
10. define additional controls.
11. calculate residual risk.
12. approve, reject, or require redesign.
13. publish a non-sensitive summary where appropriate.
14. review after material change.

## High-Risk Feature PIAs

Create separate PIAs for:

- public share analytics;
- persistent client activity history;
- client subscription and post-deletion access;
- AI assistance and model providers;
- admin impersonation or support access;
- corporate-account management;
- uploads likely to contain sensitive data;
- cross-customer analytics;
- behavioral tracking;
- deletion retention and legal holds.

## Risk Taxonomy

Assess:

- unauthorized access;
- accidental disclosure;
- identity linkage;
- excessive collection;
- unexpected reuse;
- inability to exercise rights;
- discriminatory or unfair automation;
- loss of confidentiality;
- financial or professional harm;
- exposure of estimates and commercial information;
- client relationship harm;
- privilege loss;
- vendor and cross-border risk.

## Approval Rule

No P0 or P1 residual privacy risk may be accepted solely by the implementing engineer. It requires DPO and business-owner approval, with legal review where material.
