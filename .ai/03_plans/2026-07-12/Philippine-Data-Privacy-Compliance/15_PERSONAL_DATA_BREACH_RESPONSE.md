# Personal Data Breach Response

## Objective

Create an operational incident process that can identify, contain, assess, document, and notify a personal data breach within legal deadlines.

## Incident Categories

- confidentiality breach;
- integrity breach;
- availability breach;
- lost credential;
- account takeover;
- public-link exposure;
- cross-tenant access;
- compromised admin;
- malicious insider;
- processor breach;
- accidental email disclosure;
- upload exposure;
- log or backup exposure;
- unauthorized AI-provider disclosure.

## Immediate Workflow

### 0 to 1 hour

- open incident record;
- assign incident commander;
- preserve evidence;
- contain access;
- rotate affected credentials;
- identify systems and provider;
- notify DPO.

### 1 to 12 hours

- determine affected data and subjects;
- identify whether sensitive or identity-fraud-enabling data is involved;
- estimate scale;
- determine unauthorized acquisition likelihood;
- assess serious-harm risk;
- contact processors;
- begin legal assessment;
- prepare notification draft.

### 12 to 48 hours

- validate facts;
- identify affected subjects and contact method;
- determine notification requirement;
- document reasons;
- prepare regulator and subject notifications;
- implement additional containment.

### By 72 hours where notification is required

- submit NPC notification;
- notify affected data subjects as required;
- record timing, facts, uncertainties, and follow-up commitments.

## Breach Decision Record

Record:

- discovery time;
- knowledge time;
- event timeline;
- systems;
- data categories;
- sensitive-data involvement;
- number and categories of subjects;
- acquisition likelihood;
- serious-harm assessment;
- containment;
- notification decision;
- reason;
- notification time;
- delayed-notification reason;
- processor communications;
- remediation;
- postmortem.

## Product and Engineering Requirements

- incident-management command;
- protected admin incident view;
- event timeline;
- evidence attachments;
- DPO alerts;
- processor contact registry;
- 72-hour clock;
- notification templates;
- affected-subject query;
- immutable decision log;
- post-incident action tracking.
