# Archestimator Processing Role Model

## Role Allocation Principle

Roles must be assigned by processing purpose, not by product name or contract label.

## Archestimator as Personal Information Controller

Archestimator determines the purposes and means for:

- account creation and identity records;
- authentication and session management;
- beta eligibility and onboarding;
- platform security and abuse prevention;
- support and customer communications;
- legal acceptance records;
- operational incident records;
- internal product administration;
- subscription and billing records when payments launch;
- platform analytics where Archestimator defines the purpose;
- marketing communications;
- vendor management and compliance evidence.

## Archestimator as Personal Information Processor

Archestimator generally acts under customer instructions for:

- client names and contact details entered into estimates;
- project contacts;
- shared proposals and estimate sheets;
- collaboration invitations;
- customer-authored comments and notes;
- client review, approval, and decline records;
- uploaded files containing customer-controlled data;
- customer-defined project and workspace activity;
- customer-requested exports and retention actions.

## Potential Independent or Joint Controller Scenarios

Legal approval is required before Archestimator:

- trains general models on customer data;
- creates cross-customer benchmarking;
- profiles clients or collaborators for Archestimator’s own purposes;
- retains customer data after contract termination for new product purposes;
- independently contacts customer clients for marketing;
- combines customer data with third-party datasets;
- determines legal holds unrelated to customer instructions;
- uses public share activity for unrelated monetization.

## Required Contractual Model

Customer terms and DPA must state:

- which processing Archestimator performs as controller;
- which processing Archestimator performs as processor;
- customer responsibility for lawful collection and sharing;
- Archestimator’s authorized processing instructions;
- confidentiality and security commitments;
- subprocessor use;
- data-subject assistance;
- breach notification responsibilities;
- deletion, return, retention, and backup handling;
- international processing locations;
- audit and evidence rights;
- handling of unlawful customer instructions.

## Role Decision Record

Every new feature must answer:

1. Who determines the purpose?
2. Who determines essential means?
3. Whose data subjects are affected?
4. Is processing necessary to provide the service?
5. Is data reused for Archestimator’s independent purpose?
6. What notice and contract controls apply?
