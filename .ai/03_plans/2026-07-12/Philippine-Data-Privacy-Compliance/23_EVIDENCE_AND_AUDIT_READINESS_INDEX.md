## Evidence Quality Rules

Evidence must be:

- dated;
- attributable;
- versioned;
- linked to a requirement;
- protected from unauthorized access;
- retained under an approved schedule;
- understandable to an independent reviewer;
- reproducible where technical.

## Beta Compliance Evidence Pack

Before external beta, produce:

1. signed DPO appointment;
2. NPC registration assessment;
3. approved platform PIA;
4. approved ROPA;
5. approved lawful-basis matrix;
6. approved retention schedule;
7. published notice screenshots and versions;
8. vendor register and key DPAs;
9. authorization-test report;
10. token-security report;
11. privacy-request drill;
12. deletion and retention run evidence;
13. breach tabletop;
14. admin access review;
15. release approval.

## Compliance Dashboard

The admin homebase should surface:

- ROPA review status;
- PIA review status;
- open privacy requests;
- request deadline risk;
- failed retention jobs;
- pending processor deletion;
- unresolved legal holds;
- vendor review expiry;
- subprocessor changes;
- recent privileged access;
- incidents and breach clock;
- missing evidence;
- registration renewal or review dates.
