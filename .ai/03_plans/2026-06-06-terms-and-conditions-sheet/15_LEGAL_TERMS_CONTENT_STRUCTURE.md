# Legal Terms Content Structure Checklist

> Non-final planning checklist only. A qualified lawyer must draft/review final language.

## Required sections for legal review

- Intro / acceptance of terms.
- Eligibility and account registration.
- Account security and credential responsibility.
- Subscription, payment, renewal, cancellation, refunds, taxes, invoicing — inactive for current closed beta, future-ready if billing launches.
- License to use the service.
- Ownership and intellectual property.
- User content and project data.
- Data privacy and Privacy Policy reference.
- Cookies/tracking/telemetry disclosure dependency.
- Acceptable use.
- Prohibited use.
- Third-party services/integrations/processors.
- Beta/experimental features and closed-beta status.
- AI/automated output disclaimer if Ask Flip/package-builder or other AI becomes visible.
- Estimation/output disclaimer.
- No professional advice.
- No architect-of-record, engineer-of-record, contractor, estimator-of-record, quantity surveyor, financial adviser, legal adviser, construction authority, permitting authority, code-compliance authority, or licensed professional role.
- User responsibility to review, verify, approve, and use all outputs.
- Local codes, regulations, permitting, contractor pricing, supplier pricing, labor, quantities, taxes/VAT, site conditions, market variability, project-specific assumptions, schedule and material availability disclaimers.
- Service availability / as-is / as-available.
- Changes to service.
- Changes to terms and re-acceptance.
- Termination/suspension rights.
- Warranty disclaimer.
- Limitation of liability.
- Indemnification.
- Governing law.
- Dispute resolution, arbitration/class waiver if chosen/enforceable.
- Notices.
- Contact information.
- Severability.
- Entire agreement.
- Assignment.
- Force majeure.
- Survival clauses.

## ArchEstimator-specific output checklist

Final Terms should address user responsibility for:

- estimates;
- assumptions;
- outputs;
- reports;
- templates;
- calculations;
- material quantities;
- pricing guidance;
- schedules;
- design-support information;
- recommendations;
- exports;
- workbook formulas/totals;
- proposal package outputs;
- project decisions;
- client/subcontractor/supplier communication based on product output.

## Explicit language posture for counsel to review

- “ArchEstimator is a software tool only” posture.
- “Users remain responsible” posture.
- “Independent professional review required where appropriate” posture.
- No “zero liability” or “cannot be sued” posture.
- Liability limitation and warranty disclaimers only to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law.
- Consumer/business/professional positioning consistent with actual launch.

## Current closed-beta considerations

- Current repo copy says no payment or subscription contract in beta.
- Public Terms/Privacy are closed-beta artifacts and may need full SaaS Terms before public/paid launch.
- If no billing exists, do not publish active payment/refund/cancellation terms as if subscriptions are live; include future/conditional section only if counsel approves.

## Future paid subscription considerations

If billing launches, Terms/checkout must cover:

- price and currency;
- renewal cadence;
- trial ending date if any;
- cancellation method at least as easy as signup;
- refund policy;
- taxes/VAT;
- failed payments;
- plan changes;
- downgrades/cancellation effective dates;
- data retention after cancellation;
- invoice/receipt handling.
