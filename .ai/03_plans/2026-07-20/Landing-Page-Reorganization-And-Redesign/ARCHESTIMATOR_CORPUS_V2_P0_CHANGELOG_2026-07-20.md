# Archestimator Corpus V2 — P0 Integration Changelog

**Date:** 20 July 2026  
**Source:** Archestimator Brand, Business & Startup Corpus V1 + YC/a16z Strategic Audit + Beyond YC/a16z V2 Enhancement Blueprint

## Outcome

Version 2.0 converts the corpus from a comprehensive brand encyclopedia into a more executable company operating system. The company’s full AEC market remains intact, while the product, GTM, metrics, and pitch now share one sharp opening workflow.

## P0 changes applied

1. **Canonical company core created**
   - New companion file: `ARCHESTIMATOR_CORE_V2_2026-07-20.md`.

2. **Wide-market, sharp-wedge architecture installed**
   - True market: full AEC cost-decision network.
   - Opening workflow: estimate → proposal → feedback → revision → decision.

3. **Magic moment and First Impact defined**
   - Magic moment: recipient responds through the proposal and the team revises in context.
   - First Impact: real recipient understands, questions, comments, requests revision, or decides.

4. **Why-now narrative added**
   - Persistent spreadsheet/document fragmentation;
   - rising browser-based collaboration expectations;
   - modern cloud infrastructure;
   - future intelligence from structured private context without AI dependency.

5. **Stakeholder architecture added**
   - Daily user, champion, workspace owner, economic buyer, approver, recipient, blocker.

6. **Category Entry Point system added**
   - Ten recurring buying situations and their proof assets.

7. **Switching-forces model added**
   - Push, pull, habit, anxiety;
   - adoption doctrine: meet the industry where it works today, then remove fragmentation.

8. **Formal positioning canvas installed**
   - Competitive alternatives;
   - differentiated capabilities;
   - value themes;
   - best-fit operating conditions;
   - market category and explanatory frame;
   - causal positioning statement.

9. **Validation and proof system upgraded**
   - Twelve-level evidence ladder;
   - First Impact record standard;
   - PMF-survey eligibility;
   - governed proof library and refresh dates.

10. **Metrics rebuilt around causal value**
    - North star: weekly active connected cost workflows;
    - activation, handoff, First Impact, closed loop, retention, expansion.

11. **GTM and sales upgraded**
    - First ten completed workflows;
    - SPICED discovery;
    - design-partner success plan;
    - recipient exposure, template acquisition, concierge migration, founder expertise.

12. **Pricing doctrine loosened and operationalized**
    - Candidate value metrics;
    - pricing interview guide;
    - paid-pilot rule;
    - packaging evidence register.

13. **Pitch system rebuilt**
    - Updated 15-slide master deck;
    - YC 10-slide version;
    - a16z 13-slide version;
    - customer and accelerator variants.

14. **Corpus governance added**
    - Decision record schema;
    - review triggers;
    - update cadence;
    - contradiction-resolution rules.
