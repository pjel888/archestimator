## 1. What Archestimator is

> **Archestimator is construction estimating software—an estimate-to-proposal workspace built around reusable cost intelligence.**

**User outcome**

> **Archestimator makes construction costs easier to build, communicate, review, and act on—without losing context between the first estimate and the final decision.**

**Core benefit**

> **Less rework. Clearer proposals. Better cost decisions.**

**Brand belief**

> **An estimate should not become disconnected from the proposal, the client conversation, or the revisions that follow.**

**Working promise**

> **Keep the estimate, proposal, and conversation connected.**

---

## 2. Market and opening workflow

**True market**

Archestimator serves the full AEC cost-decision network: estimators, architects, engineers, project managers, construction managers, contractors, design-build teams, commercial interiors, proposal owners, suppliers, clients, developers, and procurement.

Do not narrow the company to one profession. Do not force the entire role list into the hero.

**Shared object**

> **The construction estimate and everything that happens to it afterward.**

**Strategic rule**

> **The market is wide. The opening workflow is sharp.**

**Initial workflow wedge**

> **Archestimator first owns the moment when a construction estimate becomes a client-facing proposal, receives feedback, and must be revised without losing the number, context, or conversation.**

**MVP loop**

> **Assemble → reuse → export or publish → share → receive feedback or a decision → revise in context → reuse what was learned.**

---

## 3. Magic moment and First Impact

**Magic moment**

> **A client responds through the shared proposal and the team can revise the estimate without rebuilding the document or moving the conversation back into disconnected files.**

**First Impact**

> **A real recipient understands, questions, comments on, requests a revision to, or decides on a proposal created through Archestimator.**

A first valid total proves that the engine works. First Impact proves differentiated external value. A closed-loop revision proves the connected-workflow thesis.

**Headline product metric**

> **Weekly active connected cost workflows**

A qualifying workflow includes meaningful estimate creation or revision activity and progress toward a client-facing handoff, response, or decision.

---

## 4. Why now

AEC cost workflows remain heavily spreadsheet- and document-led, while clients and project teams increasingly expect browser-based access, clearer collaboration, and faster decisions. Modern cloud infrastructure can connect the working estimate to external review without forcing the industry to abandon familiar outputs such as Excel. The structured, private project context created by this workflow also provides a foundation for future intelligence, while the current product creates value without depending on AI.

---

## 5. Best-fit operating conditions

The market remains broad, but the current product is most valuable when the user or organization:

- repeatedly creates itemized construction estimates;
- relies on spreadsheets and files;
- converts estimates into client-facing output;
- experiences questions, clarifications, approvals, or revisions;
- needs Excel compatibility;
- has reusable costs, suppliers, components, templates, or structures;
- cares about professional presentation and traceability;
- has a real project and critical event to test.

Common critical events include a bid deadline, client cost presentation, tender submission, budget approval, scope change, change-order discussion, procurement review, or team-standardization initiative.

## 7. Positioning

**Formal positioning statement**

> **For AEC professionals and teams whose estimates become client-facing proposals and repeated revisions, Archestimator is construction estimating software that keeps the working estimate, Excel output, client review, and cost conversation connected. Unlike copied spreadsheets, detached documents, and generic project tools, it preserves reusable cost knowledge and keeps feedback attached to the number.**

**Strategic territory**

> **A focused, accessible estimate-to-proposal workspace that keeps the working estimate, familiar Excel output, client review, and revision context connected—while accumulating the user’s private reusable cost knowledge.**

**Long-term internal ambition**

> **Become the system of work for construction cost decisions.**

This ambition is internal and investor-oriented until earned through retained workflow ownership.

---

## 8. Adoption doctrine

> **Meet the industry where it works today, then remove the fragmentation around it.**

Excel is not the enemy. The enemy is the fragmentation that begins when the estimate becomes a proposal, conversation, revision, and decision.

Reduce switching risk through:

- a real prior estimate;
- familiar Excel output;
- concierge migration;
- deterministic user-controlled calculations;
- low-friction client review;
- visible product limitations and data boundaries;
- direct founder onboarding.

## 10. Current product and public boundary

Current product proof includes structured estimate authoring, reusable cost knowledge, templates, Excel preview and export, client-facing shared proposals, comments, decisions, activity, and access controls, subject to current repository verification.

For the next twelve months, the primary public story should remain focused on construction estimating, reusable knowledge, Excel, client review, feedback, revisions, and cost decisions.

Do not lead with:

- supplier marketplace or quote requests;
- procurement or payment execution;
- owner-side portfolio systems;
- broad non-AEC positioning;
- AI as a current product;
- autonomous estimating;
- fabricated metrics, traction, ROI, customers, or testimonials.

Customer data must not be described as pooled into a cross-customer estimate database.
