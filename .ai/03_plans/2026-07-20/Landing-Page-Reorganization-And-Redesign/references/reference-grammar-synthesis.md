# Reference Grammar Synthesis

## 1. Hierarchy

The references do not have equal weight.

| Reference | Weight | Controls | Cannot control |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Attio | Primary | Section order, 24-column geometry, centered hero, product-window dominance, type scale, sticky lifecycle, reveal timing, light/dark act break, responsive product treatment | Copy, brand, proof, assets, product semantics |
| Comand AI | Secondary | Technical seriousness, numbered operational principles, human-authority language, traceability/reuse framing, evidence-led case-study closure | Defense aesthetic, AI positioning, dark militaristic tone, metrics |
| Intercom | Secondary | Claim→product proof→trust cadence, mobile accordions/snap rails, calmer modular demos, explicit conversion/trust closure | Giant feature inventory, AI language, clay/blue palette, pricing mechanics |
| Archestimator corpus/source | Absolute content authority | Logo, Signal orange, copy, product facts, CTA, claim boundaries, accessibility/performance | It does not preserve stale `/new` design instructions superseded by the task directive |

If a secondary cue conflicts with Attio's structure, Attio wins. If any reference conflicts with Archestimator product truth or brand, Archestimator wins.

## 2. Attio Transfer Contract

Transfer nearly exactly:

- `48px` announcement and `68px` desktop nav;
- max-`1440px` outer frame and 24-column structural grid;
- centered hero, `64–80px` desktop H1, `40px` mobile H1, compact leading/tracking;
- generous thesis space followed by an edge-dominant product window;
- proof-grid beat after the hero;
- Platform intro followed by a long `6/24 + 18/24` sticky chapter run;
- light sections interrupted by one dark conceptual system act;
- final proof/status/CTA sequence and large structured footer;
- blur/opacity/short-translate reveal family, `600–900ms` entrance windows, `320ms` active indicator, `50ms` hover enter, `300ms` hover exit;
- horizontal sticky chapter navigation below the large breakpoint;
- fixed/readable mobile product compositions rather than squeezed dashboards.

The structural transfer is one-to-one by ordinal function:

| # | Current Attio beat | Archestimator beat |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Announcement | Design-partner announcement |
| 2 | Navigation | Fresh page-owned navigation |
| 3 | Centered hero + product composition | Canonical hero + original four-state estimate composition |
| 4 | Customer-logo grid | Source-verified capability matrix |
| 5 | Platform thesis | Connected estimate-to-decision thesis |
| 6 | Five sticky product chapters | Five connected cost-workflow chapters |
| 7 | Self-building demonstration | Explicit reusable-source selection/insertion demonstration |
| 8 | Dark Universal Context thesis | Dark connected-cost-record thesis |
| 9 | Signals | Decision-signal panes |
| 10 | Connectivity | Excel/client-portal handoffs |
| 11 | Developer platform | Structured control/traceability layer, with no API claim |
| 12 | Pinned testimonial | Founder-practitioner perspective |
| 13 | Scale proof | Four factual product principles |
| 14 | Customer story | Clearly labelled illustrative workflow using fictional data |
| 15 | Changelog | Current/partial product-status rail |
| 16 | Newsletter | Early-access briefing and FAQ with the canonical CTA path |
| 17 | Dark final CTA | Dark early-access close |
| 18 | Categorized footer | Fresh categorized footer using verified routes only |

No ordinal beat is merged, skipped, or moved. A substitute preserves spacing, hierarchy, and interaction role while refusing unsupported reference proof.

Deliberate deviations:

- no Attio customer logo wall, metrics, testimonial, or customer story because Archestimator has no verified equivalent;
- no `96px` dark heading or giant statement beyond the user's `80px` ceiling;
- no `250svh` pinned testimonial without a real customer quote;
- no canvas/video/asset density or perpetual ambient loops;
- no Attio blue; Signal orange replaces the role of the reference accent;
- no agentic/CRM language, trademarked “Universal Context,” or Attio UI replica.
- no false logo, metric, testimonial, customer, integration, or developer-platform claim inside the corresponding preserved beat.

## 3. Comand Transfer Contract

Borrow only:

- the progression from vision → operating principles → platform → numbered solutions → interoperability/trust → real evidence;
- concise human-control language as a model for Archestimator's “user remains responsible” and traceability posture;
- visible numbering and operational labels where they improve product comprehension;
- the idea that work saved in the platform becomes queryable/reusable, translated strictly into verified reusable cost knowledge.

Do not borrow:

- defense imagery, military vocabulary, national-security tone, AI-native claims, “unfair advantage,” security-grade assertions, deployment metrics, case-study proof, or Comand's brand system.

## 4. Intercom Transfer Contract

Borrow only:

- claim → product proof → trust proof cadence;
- purpose-built mobile alternatives for dense desktop UI;
- native snap rails and native FAQ disclosures;
- offscreen autoplay pause and user-interaction stop behavior;
- clear early-access closure that answers buyer questions without adding a competing conversion route.

Do not borrow:

- the full feature inventory, pricing/trial mechanics, generic AI positioning, customer photography, logos, testimonials, or blue/clay palette.

## 5. Resulting Archestimator Direction

The page should feel like Attio's current architectural marketing system translated into construction cost work: disciplined white grid, compact Inter typography, restrained Signal atmosphere, large original product proof, long connected-workflow chapters, one dark system explanation, and truthful evidence in place of startup traction.

It must not feel like:

- the current beige/night/WebGL `/new`;
- a generic card/bento SaaS template;
- an AI landing page;
- an Attio clone containing Archestimator text;
- a dense app screenshot dump;
- a visual concept that overstates the live revision loop.

## 6. Originality Test

A reviewer should be able to identify Attio's page grammar from the hierarchy and motion, but every word, fixture, diagram, product surface, color accent, proof claim, and conversion action must be independently attributable to Archestimator. If an element depends on a reference asset or reference-specific business claim, remove or replace it.
