# Comand AI Homepage Grammar — Secondary Study

**Observed:** 2026-07-20 on the live [Comand AI homepage](https://www.comand.ai/). Comand is a secondary cue only; it does not control the Archestimator page order, type system, or motion system.

## Current Narrative

1. Minimal navigation and demo CTA.
2. Large mission-led hero and manifesto film.
3. Long vision statement: context should compound rather than be reassembled.
4. Operational principles: human command, stack interoperability, reusable mission knowledge, security.
5. Platform thesis.
6. Numbered solution selector with a large output-oriented product story.
7. Interoperability/security proof.
8. Deployment case study with attributed quote and measured outcomes.
9. Newsroom and structured footer.

## Useful Secondary Cues

- Treat principles as operating contracts rather than icon-card features.
- Number multi-step solutions clearly.
- Keep human authority explicit and separate analysis from decision authority.
- Explain reuse as accumulated, queryable work—not vague “intelligence”.
- Put real proof after the product explanation and label outputs concretely.
- Use terse labels and large technical compositions to communicate seriousness.

## Archestimator Translation

- “Human command” becomes user responsibility: Archestimator structures and preserves cost context; professionals still verify and own every estimate, output, and decision.
- “Context compounds” becomes source-verified reusable cost knowledge and preserved proposal/review context.
- Numbered solutions reinforce the five Attio-led workflow chapters.
- Output labels should name actual estimate, Excel, proposal, comment, and decision states.

## Do Not Transfer

No defense or battlefield vocabulary, military imagery, AI-native positioning, unfair-advantage rhetoric, defense-grade security claim, integrations claim, deployment logo, testimonial, or measured outcome may transfer. The site supplies precision and authority cues only.
