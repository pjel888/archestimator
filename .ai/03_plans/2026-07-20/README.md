## The 2026-07-21 deep trace — three material discoveries

The Improve-Plan-Prompt's "explore facets we haven't discovered yet" directive
produced three findings that reshaped the packs:

1. **Pack D reframe (most significant).** The original "extract MutationIntent
   primitives from `rows.ts`" was wrong. The deep write-path trace found **9
   forked write paths** (not 4) and confirmed the shared primitives **already
   exist** in the `d1-intents/` family (`database.ts`, `history-events.ts`,
   `intent-context.ts`, `access.ts`) plus `mutation-receipts.ts` and
   `project-component-attachment-retention.ts`. The work is routing forks
   through existing primitives. The single biggest integrity hole: **5 forks
   silently drop the `activity_event_outbox` row** when they hand-roll the
   `estimate_sheet_events` INSERT — their mutations never reach the workspace
   activity feed. UNIT-001 closes that hole first. Full primitive surface +
   fork gap matrix in `Estimate-Mutation-Integrity-Unification/references/primitive-map.md`.
2. **ARCH-05 is a false positive (closed).** The component-library `load*`
   actions are thin at the action layer but every one delegates to a repository
   function scoped by `WHERE workspace_id = ?` from `ensureDefaultWorkspace()`.
   No IDOR. No new unit. Documented in Pack A's README.
3. **Pack E added (money-math).** The trace found the canonical oracle
   (`computeEstimateRowCostParts`) is dependency-free but lives in
   `lib/estimates/`, creating ARCH-08 (upward repository deps). **4
   user-facing surfaces display float-derived totals**, the AI budget ignores
   quantity entirely, and there are **11 currency formatter definitions** (the
   prior audit said 8). Pack E relocates the oracle to a neutral leaf, flips
   the 4 surfaces, and consolidates the formatters. Full map in
   `Money-Math-Domain-Module/references/money-math-map.md`.
