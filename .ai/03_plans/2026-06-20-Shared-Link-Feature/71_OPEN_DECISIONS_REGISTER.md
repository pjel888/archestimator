## How to resolve

For each row: pick the option (or confirm the recommendation), record it in `.ai/10_state/decisions.md`
with a one-line rationale and date, then change Status here to **Resolved** and update the dependent doc.
**Beta cannot be declared ready until D1–D5 and D8–D9 are at least Recommended-accepted** (they gate
privacy, security, and deploy correctness); D6/D7/D10/D11 may ship as recorded deferrals. See
[99](99_QUALITY_SCORECARD_AND_SIGNOFF.md) for the launch gate.
