## Transition Rule

A requirement remains `PLANNED` when any owning active unit lacks a current verified evidence link. Evidence status `VERIFIED` means complete, current, schema/dependency-valid, durably indexed evidence; for the `UNIT-001` through `UNIT-003` baseline cohort it does not rewrite a measured `overall=FAIL`. After `UNIT-002` changes any path in its closed repair map, the preceding `UNIT-001` cohort is stale until an immutable repair/refreeze receipt joins the predecessor and refreshed application/producer hashes and the complete `UNIT-001` sequence commits a current `EVID-002` projection. `EVID-005`, `EVID-007`, `EVID-008`, `EVID-009`, and `EVID-010` cannot become `VERIFIED` without their required immutable browser-report and fixture-bound E2E-receipt children carrying the matching fixed `browserScope`. A requirement becomes runtime `PASS` only when every required active unit is `VERIFIED` or decision-backed `SUPERSEDED`, every listed hard gate is green under the enforcing policy, and the evidence index contains matching source/build/input hashes plus every required repair, cleanup, and child-dependency receipt or a validated artifact-only `NOT_APPLICABLE` declaration. `SKIPPED` requires a reason and residual risk and cannot satisfy a hard budget. No prose edit changes runtime status; `plan-pack.json` is the authoritative journaled commit point.
