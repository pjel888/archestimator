# Landing-reference synthesis: transferable design DNA, not a clone

**Research status:** complete, first-party live-page research only.  
**Retrieved:** 2026-07-23 (UTC+08:00). Live marketing sites are mutable; this is a dated visual/content reference, not a claim about a permanent version.  
**Method boundary:** inspected public HTML and publicly served CSS/text only. No browser automation, screenshots, interaction recording, or third-party gallery was used.

## Decision

Give Archestimator a **calm, architectural editorial shell around literal product proof**:

1. an airy light canvas, dark ink, one confident orange action color, subtle hairlines and shallow surfaces;
2. a compact, straightforward navigation and a single clear product-first hero;
3. one real estimate workspace as the hero proof, followed by a deliberately paced sequence of real workflow moments;
4. dense, specific construction language in short blocks, with only the right amount of supporting detail;
5. restrained motion that clarifies state and priority instead of performing “AI” or imitating any reference.

This combines the references' strongest shared idea: a landing page should make the product's point of view immediately legible, then earn belief with concrete proof. It must not reproduce a reference's copy, trademark, visual asset, illustration style, component geometry, screen composition, or animation choreography.

## Evidence legend

- **Observed** means present in the retrieved first-party page or its publicly served source.
- **Inference for Archestimator** is a recommendation derived from multiple observations; it is not a fact about a reference site.
- “Light/airy” is only asserted where content/source establishes a light surface, limited palette, or generous whitespace. No pixel-perfect visual claims are made without visual inspection.

## Per-site findings

### Intercom: outcome first, then product-system proof

**Observed**

- The homepage starts with a compact product/navigation layer, then the headline “The only helpdesk designed for the AI Agent era,” a short supporting paragraph, and two actions: free trial and demo. It follows with a list of product/system concepts, decorative images, and product imagery. [Homepage](https://www.intercom.com/)
- Its subsequent page is not a generic feature dump: headings group the story around agent workspace, customer/agent continuity, insights, and self-improvement, with product UI and decorative media punctuating the sections. [Homepage feature sequence](https://www.intercom.com/)
- The closing region repeats the core outcome and both main actions, then uses a large, structured feature/footer index. [Homepage closing and footer](https://www.intercom.com/)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- Use one compact hero thesis, a supporting sentence that names the real estimating work it changes, and two intentionally different actions (for example, primary product access and secondary “see an estimate built”).
- Treat decoration as a pacing device around real interface evidence. For Archestimator, project/estimate artefacts, plan fragments, cost logic, and actual interface states are more truthful than stock “construction inspiration.”

### Fathom: credibility comes from the domain's native artefacts

**Observed**

- The homepage leads with a category claim, explicitly names its served sectors, offers “Book a demo” and a product-stack route, then presents current papers, reports, product launches, and map/data imagery. [Homepage hero and current material](https://www.fathom.global/)
- It divides the page into products, sectors, knowledge/research, partners, and company material. Products are introduced in a small set of high-level groups rather than an unbounded grid. [Homepage navigation and product sections](https://www.fathom.global/)
- The live content pairs each editorial item with a type label such as “New report,” “Research,” “New product,” or “Product briefing.” [Homepage update stream](https://www.fathom.global/)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- Use the construction equivalent of native evidence: an estimate line item, a scope decision, a takeoff/plan context, a cost-library selection, a proposal outcome. Labeling a small number of evidence modules (“Estimate workspace,” “Scope,” “Cost intelligence,” “Proposal”) can make a long page scannable without generic cards.
- Do not borrow Fathom's sector breadth. Archestimator should lead with a specific professional job to be done, not an abstract claim of industry leadership.

### Mistral: platform authority through hierarchy and modularity

**Observed**

- The live hero repeats the compact message “Frontier AI. In your hands.” and follows it with a short mission statement and featured news. [Homepage hero](https://mistral.ai/)
- The page establishes social proof with a row of recognizable customer logos and linked industry stories, then organizes the offer into a small product family with one-line explanations and capability bullets. [Homepage customer and product sequence](https://mistral.ai/)
- Its nav and footer expose a large product/solutions/research/developer/company universe, appropriate to a multi-product AI company. [Homepage navigation](https://mistral.ai/)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- Borrow the discipline of a large, controlled headline and modular product narrative, not its scale. Archestimator needs one product story; the navigation should be markedly simpler than Mistral's.
- Only show customer logos, numeric outcomes, or partner marks that Archestimator has the rights and evidence to publish. If unavailable, use product-derived proof instead.

### Rows: the strongest interaction and small-grid reference

**Observed**

- Rows explicitly presents itself as an “unapologetically simple” shared list. Its page begins with a logo, a short title/subtitle, and only “Enjoy the demo” plus “Request an invite,” then tells a series of product stories. [Homepage](https://rows.gg/)
- Each story couples a tight heading and short, voice-led explanation with a mini product scene. The public source establishes a 720px maximum content column; at desktop, a two-column grid puts the proof scene beside the copy, with a 248px copy column and a 16:10 media surface. On narrow screens the source switches to a stacked presentation with media first. [Publicly served homepage source](https://rows.gg/)
- The public copy names interaction details instead of merely claiming delight: live cursors, personalised “New” state, complete/kill feedback, row lifting, keyboard access, and optional sound. Public source also contains initial opacity/translate/blur states for the staged scenes, so entrance transitions are evidenced. [Homepage](https://rows.gg/)
- The public CSS names a warm off-white `#f5f5f2` surface, dark `#1a191d` ink, muted `#e8e8e3` scene surfaces, a low-contrast hairline, Suisse/Inter/system sans, and 5px media corners. These values are reference evidence, **not** Archestimator tokens. [Publicly served stylesheet](https://rows.gg/_next/static/chunks/3xky-yrtiwf7f.css?dpl=dpl_6DECP2gUM2FhJpvK8HuuFcnvpz6Y)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- This is the right pattern for the landing page's mid-body: alternate a real, focused estimate-workspace crop with a 2–4 sentence explanation of one user-visible gain. The proof should show true Archestimator UI, not a Rows-like recreation.
- Motion budget: use a short entrance only for proof modules, direct manipulation feedback where it exists in the product, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Never add Rows' delightful destruction/spark/sound treatments merely to make a B2B estimate workflow feel animated.

### Agentwork: spare structure and evidence-bearing examples

**Observed**

- The homepage begins with one sentence of product promise and an inline set of four links rather than a full menu. Its first content section is a literal three-step “Integrate / Ask / Improves” explanation. [Homepage](https://agentwork.com/)
- The following sections prove the proposition through realistic user questions, source verification, permissions, integrations, and FAQ material. [Homepage workflow, trust, and FAQ](https://agentwork.com/)
- Its CSS publicly declares a muted “sand” background system, an orange accent, Mona Sans as the default sans, tight heading tracking, small radii, and ordinary short color/background transitions. This supports a restrained, text-forward material read; it does **not** establish glass effects. [Public stylesheet](https://agentwork.com/static/assets/main-CkfR9ZYb.css)
- The page itself states that some security work is “in progress.” That is a useful evidence discipline: do not turn planned capability into present-tense marketing proof. [Homepage security section](https://agentwork.com/)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- Explain the product with a short human sequence such as “Start an estimate / Build the cost / Send the decision,” supported by actual interface evidence. Use real project-language prompts/outcomes only if the feature exists.
- Agentwork's sparseness is a good counterweight to enterprise-feature sprawl. Its one-page information architecture is too text-led to be a standalone visual blueprint for Archestimator.

### Taito.ai: the closest high-level landing-page structure

**Observed**

- The navigation groups Product and Solutions, then exposes Pricing, Blog, Company, Log in, and Join waitlist. The hero adds a small category label, a concise outcome headline, one supporting paragraph, one primary action, and a literal illustrated app frame with visible navigation and task content. [Homepage navigation and hero](https://taito.ai/)
- Immediately after the hero app proof, the page uses a logo strip and then a “Why” section, individual workflow narratives, a product capability list, automation/workflow proof, role-based sections, testimonial, security, integrations, quantified claims, FAQ, a closing CTA, and a dark multi-column footer. [Homepage](https://taito.ai/)
- Its proof modules show specific input and output, not empty device mockups: an onboarding message followed by concrete system actions, time-off policy entries, a workflow checklist, review-source labels, and integration surfaces. [Homepage workflow proof](https://taito.ai/)
- The public source declares Suisse Intl as the primary font, a light surface system, small rounded controls, shallow shadows, and a dark footer. Its markup uses horizontally responsive grids for the footer. [Public homepage source](https://taito.ai/)

**Inference for Archestimator**

- Use Taito's structural principle: **claim → real app proof → why this changes the work → focused workflow proof → credibility → closing conversion**. Compress it substantially for Archestimator; do not create an HRIS-length page.
- A real estimate workspace should carry the hero. If an exact present product state is not viable, use an explicitly labelled concept or omit it; a generic fake product UI would conflict with the desired proof standard.

## Proposed Archestimator information architecture

This is a planning recommendation, not a claim that every reference uses this exact order.

1. **Compact top navigation** — mark; a small set of routes only; primary orange CTA. No mega-menu unless actual public-route content needs it.
2. **Hero: one construction-estimating outcome** — small context label, large outcome headline, one supporting sentence naming the work, primary CTA and a lower-emphasis secondary route.
3. **Literal hero proof** — an exact current Archestimator estimate workspace, framed as a calm shallow surface rather than faux browser chrome. Caption only what is actually on screen.
4. **Short credibility rail** — customer proof only if authorised; otherwise real product qualities or carefully sourced measurable facts. Never invent project/customer metrics.
5. **Three focused workflow proofs** — a lightweight alternating grid: estimate start/scope, cost-library decision, client/proposal-ready output. One exact product proof each, one concise benefit each.
6. **Why it fits the job** — one editoral section grounded in AEC/estimating constraints, not a broad “AI platform” list.
7. **Trust/consequence section** — only support, collaboration, data, or workflow claims that can be verified in product/current policy. Use plain text and links rather than a fictitious compliance badge field.
8. **Closing CTA and compact footer** — restate the action with minimal route groups, legal links, and contact/support paths.

### Layout and material rules

- **Desktop first:** establish a wide outer grid and a quieter reading measure for copy. Use the Rows-like alternating proof/copy rhythm only as a principle, with Archestimator's own columns, breakpoints, and tokens.
- **Light/airy system:** warm white or quiet neutral background, near-black text, intentional negative space, fine structural borders, and an orange CTA fill from `--brand-orange`. Use surface contrast to make the product workspace primary.
- **Typography:** a compact, high-contrast sans hierarchy with tight but readable display tracking; small labels, short paragraphs, and clear evidence captions. Do not copy Suisse Intl or Mona Sans merely because the references use them; use the project's licensed/available typography.
- **Glass/depth:** no evidence supports using glass as the central language. Prefer solid paper-like panels, hairlines, and one or two shallow shadows. A translucent layer is acceptable only when it communicates actual hierarchy, not as decorative “AI glass.”
- **Grid division:** keep section ownership legible: editorial copy, then product proof, rather than every section becoming a rounded-card mosaic. On small screens, show the relevant proof before its explanation where that reduces ambiguity.
- **Copy density:** hero is one proposition plus one supporting sentence. Product sections can be specific, but cap the explanation at a short heading, two or three sentences, and a proof caption. Reserve dense detail for a clearly optional lower-page layer or linked pages.

### Motion and scroll rules

- **Supported by first-party evidence:** Rows exposes staged scene entrances and calls out real micro-interactions/sound; Fathom exposes a stream of editorial/product tiles; the other pages use repeated product/media modules. These support pacing with a small number of purposeful reveals, not a mandate for animation. [Rows](https://rows.gg/) [Fathom](https://www.fathom.global/)
- **Archestimator recommendation:** one brief opacity/translate reveal per independently meaningful proof block; no autoplay motion required to understand the product; no parallax, ambient particles, typewriter copy, scrolling logo marquees, faux AI generation, or animation that hides the actual estimate workspace. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` with stable content.
- **Interaction rule:** motion must reveal real state (row selected, scope expanded, estimate updated) or support orientation. Decorative animation gets cut first if it competes with proof, performance, or accessibility.

## Explicit non-copy boundaries

1. Do not recreate any reference page's DOM order, distinctive two-column measurements, header, footer, hero wording, card silhouette, illustration, photographs, product UI, logo strip, or brand palette.
2. Do not use another company's name, customer logo, testimonial, metric, security/compliance claim, or integration icon as Archestimator evidence.
3. Do not turn the page into an AI-company landing page. “AI” should appear only where it truthfully describes a present Archestimator capability and never replace the construction-estimating value proposition.
4. Do not substitute generic dashboard mockups for exact software. Product proof is either real current UI or explicitly marked concept material.
5. Do not copy Rows' playful destructive interaction language or sound treatment, Intercom's lifestyle image collage, Fathom's technical map imagery, Mistral's institutional platform breadth, Agentwork's source/security claims, or Taito's HR workflow/copy.
6. Do not make the navigation/footer more complex than the public information architecture actually requires.

## Reusable takeaway

**Archestimator should feel like a clear estimating workspace made visible in daylight:** one strong outcome, exact product proof early, calm editorial pacing, native construction artefacts, and only restrained motion/depth. The reference combination is a discipline of clarity and proof, not an aesthetic template to reproduce.
