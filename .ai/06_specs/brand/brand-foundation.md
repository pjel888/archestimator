## Positioning

Archestimator is an AEC product. Full stop. It is a construction cost estimating platform whose deeper value is the **cost intelligence layer** for the people who build and approve cost estimates and cost proposals across a project.

The category stack:

| Depth | Label | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | The cost intelligence layer | Evoke curiosity; name the deepest value |
| **Anchor** | Construction cost estimating platform | Say what we are; be searchable |
| **Ambition** | The supplier-linked cost intelligence layer for AEC teams | Investor/partner framing |

The hook must always carry an AEC/construction qualifier in public, because the bare phrase “cost intelligence” is owned by CloudZero in cloud/FinOps and the concept is established in enterprise construction estimating. The defensible mark is **Archestimator**, not the phrase.

## Primary audience (AEC-committed)

Archestimator serves the project cost ecosystem in the Philippines and Southeast Asia first: everyone who builds a cost estimate or cost proposal, and everyone who reviews and approves one.

### Tier 1 — the entry wedge
- Contractors / general contractors
- Cost estimators / quantity surveyors
- Specialty and fit-out firms / subcontractors
- Architects

### Tier 2 — expand once the wedge holds
- Engineers (civil / structural / MEP)
- Suppliers / material and product vendors
- Interior designers / design-build studios

### Tier 3 — the second side (strategic top-down loop)
- Clients / owners / developers
- Project managers / construction managers / owner’s reps / PMO
- Procurement / finance (client side)

The product can technically estimate anything with line items, but every word of marketing, every feature priority, and the first cohort of users point at AEC.

## Core user pain

The quote/proposal workflow lives in spreadsheets, so cost logic is rebuilt per project, revisions break files, assumptions disappear, and nobody (proposer or client) holds a reliable record of what was submitted, changed, and approved.

The deeper pain is normalized waste. Many teams do not realize how much time they lose to re-finding, rechecking, rebuilding, and reconnecting work that should have stayed connected.

## Brand promise

> You never start from zero again. Your numbers hold up.

This captures both benefits: time (stop rebuilding) and intelligence/defensibility (your work compounds and you can defend every number).

## Mission, vision, promise

**Mission (proposed, awaiting ratification):** Give AEC teams a cost intelligence layer, one place where unit costs, suppliers, revisions, and client decisions compound instead of dying in spreadsheets, from first cost estimate to client approval.

**Vision (proposed):** Become the cost intelligence layer for the AEC economy: every estimate a reusable asset, every approval a permanent record, and a structured bridge from project demand to supplier supply.

**Brand promise:** You never start from zero again. Your numbers hold up.

**Final brand rule:** Archestimator should not sound like hype. It should sound like leverage.

## Benefit pillars

1. **Never start from zero** — reusable component library: your prices, suppliers, brands, variants, and cost entries.
2. **Your numbers hold up** — Activity, Node Activity, and row version history keep every revision traceable.
3. **From estimate to approval** — shared publication links with comments, decisions, and password protection.
4. **Built the way AEC teams think** — project hierarchy, area groups, and discipline-aware structure.
5. **Client-side control** — a shared record of submissions, revisions, comments, and approvals (live sharing today; persistent client archive as planned direction).

## Product truth to preserve

- The estimate sheet is the center of gravity.
- The Component Library supports faster, more consistent pricing across projects.
- A project can contain multiple estimates for options, alternates, revisions, and cost proposals.
- Version control matters because estimating data is too important to lose in file drift.
- Visual cost breakdowns matter because clients need to understand cost differences, not only see totals.
- Revisions matter because cost proposals change repeatedly before approval.
- Proposal output matters because the estimate must become something a client can understand and approve.
- Trust matters more than pure automation.
- The user’s problem is not lack of skill; it is too much skilled work trapped inside disconnected tools.

## What Archestimator is not

- Not a broad ERP, procurement execution, inventory, accounting, or project-management system.
- Not a generic quoting tool for “any quote-heavy business.”
- Not an invoice-only app or generic one-line quote form; Archestimator is for structured, itemized cost estimates and proposal packages where assumptions, pricing, revisions, and handoff matter.
- Not an AI estimator that replaces estimator judgment; AI is deferred from launch.
- Not a design/BIM takeoff platform unless that scope is explicitly added later.
- Not an aggregator or database of external estimates.

## Differentiation (honest, re-grounded)

1. **Built for the teams enterprise tools ignore.** Reusable cost intelligence, finally simple and affordable enough for spreadsheet-bound SMBs, suppliers, fit-out shops, and solo estimators in PH and SEA.
2. **Estimate to approval, not just estimate.** Shared proposals with comments, decisions, password protection, and a client-side record, past where most estimating tools stop.
3. **The supplier-linked bridge.** Estimate → supplier catalog → quote request → procurement. The genuinely unclaimed part.
4. **Private by design.** Your data is yours, never pooled or anonymized across customers.
5. **Founder-market fit.** Built by an architect / renderer / UX-UI operator / former software engineer who has lived the gap.
6. **Honesty as brand.** Live/Partial/Planned/Not-Yet discipline; a truth posture in a vapor-heavy market.

Do not claim “we invented reusable estimating intelligence.” Enterprise estimating tools already do that; our wedge is fit, reach, price, and connection.

## Claims requiring caution

Avoid or soften unless proven:

- “World-class”
- “AI-powered” as a leading claim
- “Trusted by” with fake logos
- Guaranteed time savings
- Guaranteed accuracy
- Claims that imply official certification or engineering/quantity-surveying authority
- “Most intuitive” when presented without nearby product proof or as a verified #1 market claim
- Problem language that shames spreadsheet users or implies non-users are careless, slow, or unintelligent
- “Immutable issued record,” “legal audit trail,” “procurement system,” “payment platform,” “compliance record,” “guaranteed dispute protection”
- “Only tool that…” or any aggregator/pooled-data claim

## Approved language

Use often: cost intelligence (layer), cost estimate, cost proposal, BOQ, DUPA, unit costs, proposal-ready numbers, reusable cost logic, revision context, decision-ready proposals, commercial control, defensible estimates, AEC teams, proposal handoff.

Use carefully: AI-assisted, procurement, approval workflow, enterprise, compliance, automation.

Avoid for now: ERP, full procurement platform, legal automation, purchase-order execution, industry standard, used by leading firms, guaranteed accuracy, fully automated estimating, aggregator, database of estimates, “the only tool that…”
