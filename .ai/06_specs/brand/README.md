# Brand, Narrative, and Voice

**Canonical startup corpus:** `.ai/00_corpus/ARCHESTIMATOR_MASTER.md` (v4.3, 2026-06-18) and its companion redline at `.ai/00_corpus/companion-views/archestimator-founder-redline.md`. This folder contains brand-specific derivations, but the master file is the single source of truth for brand, product, business, pricing, marketing, and operations.

## Purpose

Use this folder when refining how Archestimator explains itself to users, investors, collaborators, and future agents.

It should answer:

1. What is Archestimator?
2. Who is it for first?
3. What pain does it remove?
4. What better working state does it create?
5. What should the brand sound like?
6. What claims are allowed, softened, or avoided?
7. How should landing-page copy, product copy, and public messaging stay consistent?
8. Which secondary public claims support the hero without replacing it?
9. What hidden time waste and messy workflow is Archestimator trying to untangle?

## Current primary public claim

**Hero / hook:**

> **Your cost intelligence layer.**
> The prices, suppliers, and estimates you build in Archestimator, structured and ready for your next proposal. Quote faster. Defend every number.

**Plain-language anchor:** A construction cost estimating platform.

**Campaign tagline:** From cost proposal to client approval.

**Brand promise:** You never start from zero again. Your numbers hold up.

## Current problem frame

Archestimator should be explicit that the problem is not estimator skill. The problem is the messy workflow around skilled estimating work in AEC.

Core internal line:

> Most estimating teams do not realize how much time they lose to the work around the estimate: finding files, checking versions, rebuilding rows, hunting assumptions, comparing changes, and rewriting outputs.

Brand territory:

- untangle the mess around the cost estimate;
- bring clarity to cost estimate and cost proposal workflows;
- stop suffering through disconnected estimating and proposal workflows;
- offer a faster, clearer, better way to build itemized cost proposals;
- make the cost logic visible before it becomes a proposal or approval problem.

## Working rules

- Keep market-facing claims specific and believable.
- Do not invent testimonials, customers, certifications, or adoption proof.
- Treat fake logos/testimonials as placeholders only until replaced or removed.
- Prefer AEC and construction-estimator workflow language; the product is AEC-committed, not a generic quoting tool.
- Separate product truth from aspirational future features.
- Date time-sensitive market, pricing, or competitive claims.
- Name messy workflow pain directly, but do not shame estimators or attack Excel itself. The enemy is disconnected work, not the user.
- Obey claims discipline: Live / Partial / Planned / Not-Yet. See `ARCHESTIMATOR_MASTER.md` §15.
- Do not feature AI as a current capability; AI is deferred to the roadmap.
- Do not claim we invented reusable estimating intelligence, or that we are the only tool that does it. The master file §2 documents established enterprise players in this space.
